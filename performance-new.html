What changed and when

About these numbers

  • 5 November: the record starts. Every tip is published before the start with its price and stake, and stays on the record.
  • 10 November: AI tips published 5 to 9 November under TOM moved to NCM. No result, price or stake changed; only the Meister label.
  • 19 November: the NCM Racing Meister starts its own record, with the exchange method.
  • 25 November: The Original Meister (TOM) and the Acca Meister retire; their tips, combos and accas stay in our full ledger and are not in these numbers. The Combo Meister becomes a full Meister, and NCM Premium Events starts.
  • Points are at the advised stake and the published price. Won–lost counts settled tips; voids and tips still to run are not counted.
