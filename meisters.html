The BetMeisters tipsters

BetMeisters tips come from four Meisters, each with its own method and its own public record since 5 November 2026.

Number Cruncher (NCM)

Value betting tips across football, tennis, basketball and more. A tip is published only when a bookmaker's price beats the fair chance from the sharp market, with the margin removed, by at least 5%. Free tips at shorter and longer prices, Pro tips between 2.00 and 5.99.

NCM Premium Events (NCMP)

The same price-first method on the biggest events: the top European football leagues and every UEFA competition, the NBA, EuroLeague, NHL and NFL, and the ATP and WTA tours. Bookmakers price these games tightly, so the edges are smaller.

NCM Racing Meister (NCMR)

Horse racing tips for UK and Irish meetings, priced against the Betfair Exchange and Matchbook. A horse is tipped when the best bookmaker price beats the fair price from the exchanges.

Combo Meister (CM)

Doubles and trebles built from legs that each beat the fair price, so the combined price carries an edge too.

Every tip shows its price, stake and our chance before the start, and every result, win or lose, stays on the results page. See today's tips.
