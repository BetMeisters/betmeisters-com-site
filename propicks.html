Pro Picks

Each day the Number Cruncher’s best Pro tips, up to five, are picked out: an edge of +6% to +12% still available at the current price, starting at least two hours later. Every Pro Pick stays on the public record, win or lose.

How Pro Picks are chosen

  • NCM Pro tips only. Each Pick is already an NCM Pro tip (priced 2.00 to 5.99, edge +5% to +14%).
  • Edge +6% to +12%, the band where our record is strongest, re-checked at the current price. Our record showed the very biggest edges were more often price errors, so they are not picked (from 20 Nov).
  • At most two per sport, and at least two hours before the start, so a single sport can’t carry the day.
  • One label per tip. A Pro Pick is never also a NAP or a Meister Special; if a tip moves up to NAP, the next qualifying Pro tip takes its place.
  • 1 point each, at the published price. The record below counts every Pick, winners and losers.
