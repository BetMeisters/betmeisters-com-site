Every Number Cruncher tip, Combo Meister combo and Acca Meister acca is only published when its price beats the fair price.

    How it works. The fair chance comes from the sharp market's prices with the bookmaker margin removed. Fair price = 1 ÷ fair chance. Value = your price × fair chance − 1: above 0% the price beats the market's view, below 0% the bookmaker keeps that share of your stake over many bets like it. An acca multiplies the chances, so it multiplies the margin too. Two legs from the same game are linked, so the checker takes one leg per game. A guide, not a tip: prices and chances move.
