How to read this page

Last price is the horse's most recent listed price before it was withdrawn. Rule 4 is the deduction from winnings on other horses in the race, for bets struck at a fixed price before the withdrawal, using the Tattersalls Rule 4(c) scale. It is indicative: bookmakers apply the scale to the withdrawn horse's price at the moment it came out, and several withdrawals are added together (never more than 90p in the pound). Starting price bets are not affected. A single on the non-runner itself is void and the stake comes back.

Withdrawn horse's price at the time it came out. Deduction in pence per pound of winnings (Rule 4 is set on fractional prices; decimal and US are the same bands converted). US odds of −100 to −105 and +100 count as about evens.

Each way: place terms follow the number of runners, so a withdrawal can change them: 5 to 7 runners pay 2 places, 8 or more pay 3, and handicaps with 16 or more pay 4. Fewer than 5 runners is win only. Bookmakers settle each-way bets on the number of runners that actually start, and some offer extra places as a promotion.
