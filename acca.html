How an acca is priced. Each leg has our fair chance from the sharpest markets. The acca's chance is all the leg chances multiplied together, and its edge is that chance times the acca price, minus one. Every leg must beat its fair price, or be close to it and confirmed by a second price. Asian lines can half-win or be refunded; a refunded leg drops out and the acca pays on the rest. 18+ · BeGambleAware.org
