Football tips

Football is where most of our tips come from. Every tip starts from the sharp market: we take the prices of the bookmakers that serious bettors use, remove the margin to get a fair chance for every outcome, and only tip when a UK bookmaker's price beats that fair chance by a clear amount (at least 5%).

We tip the match result, Asian handicaps (including quarter and whole lines) and goal totals. Leagues are graded before we tip them, so lower divisions only appear when their prices are reliable, and youth and reserve matches are left out unless the edge is checked twice.

Every tip shows the price we took, the bookmaker, the fair price and the edge, and after the match we record the closing price. Beating the closing price is the best sign a method works, so we publish how often we do it.

How our tips work

We remove the bookmaker margin from the sharp market to find a fair chance, and tip only when a UK price beats it by enough to matter. Stakes are in points so the record compares like for like. We never take affiliate money, and losing tips stay on the record. Read how we price a tip and see which UK bookmaker has the best odds.
