Horse racing tips

Our NCM Racing Meister prices every runner from the betting exchanges, with the margin removed, and tips a horse only when a UK bookmaker's price is clearly bigger than its fair chance. Each-way tips are checked against the place terms and a place model, and a favourite needs a win edge, not just a place edge.

Every racing tip is published before the off with its price, stake and place terms. Afterwards we record the Betfair starting price as the closing price, so you can see whether we beat the market's final view.

Non-runners and Rule 4 deductions change prices through the day, so we also publish a live non-runners page for UK and Irish racing.

How our tips work

We remove the bookmaker margin from the sharp market to find a fair chance, and tip only when a UK price beats it by enough to matter. Stakes are in points so the record compares like for like. We never take affiliate money, and losing tips stay on the record. Read how we price a tip and see which UK bookmaker has the best odds.
