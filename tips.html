How to read a tip

Free, Pro, NAP, Best Value
Teal is a Free tip, yellow is Pro. Orange marks the NAP, our most confident selection. Blue marks Best Value, the biggest edge of the day available at three or more bookmakers.
Chance, bookie, edge
Chance is our estimate of the true chance. Bookmaker is the chance the best price implies. Edge is chance × price − 1. Each-way tips show the win and place chance. Stakes are in points: 1 is standard.
Times
Start times are UK time (GMT in winter, BST in summer), with your local time alongside if you are outside the UK. Every day on the site is the UK day.
