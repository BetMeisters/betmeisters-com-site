The Meisters

The Original Meister (human picks), the Number Cruncher (every AI pick outside racing) and the NCM Racing Meister (the Number Cruncher's horse racing, its own record from 20 Nov). The dashed lines are the same tips settled at the closing price (in racing, the starting price; from 19 Nov the Betfair starting price), so the gap shows what getting on early is worth. Where no closing price was recorded we keep the published price.

NCM Pro v NCM Free

The Number Cruncher’s two tiers. Every NCM tip has an edge of +5% to +14%. NCM Pro tips are priced 2.00 to 5.99, the range where our record is strongest; NCM Free tips are the rest of the card (1.60 to 1.99, and 6.00 or more at half a point). The split by price applies to tips from 20 November; earlier tips keep the tier they were published with. The Original Meister’s picks are always free and have their own record.

The Combo Meisters (Combo Meister, Original Combo Meister and Racing Combo Meister) have their own records on the Combos page; they are not part of these numbers.

The Acca Meister has his own record on the Accas page; it is not part of these numbers.
