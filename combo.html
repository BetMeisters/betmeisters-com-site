How a combo is built. Main sports only (football, tennis, basketball, NFL, NHL). Each leg must be at least 50% likely, priced above our fair price from the sharpest market by at least the singles bar minus 2 points (football 7%, never under 5%), and matched by a second bookmaker or the exchange. No leg is on a game we tip as a single, no two legs share a team, and a void leg drops out so the combo pays on the rest. 18+ · BeGambleAware.org
