Privacy and cookies

What we collect, the cookies we use and your rights. Last updated 23 November 2026.

Who we are

BetMeisters.com is a sports tipping site from BetMeisters, with news, previews, guides and reviews at betmeisters.com/news. For anything about your data, email tom@betmeisters.com.

What we collect

Nothing that identifies you directly. There are no accounts, no sign-up forms and no payments on this site. Until 1 January every tip is free to read.

Cookies and analytics

We use Google Analytics 4 to count visits and see which pages people read, only if you accept analytics cookies in the banner. Until you accept, Google Analytics is not loaded and no analytics cookies are set. If you accept, Google sets cookies such as _ga and _ga_* that last up to 2 years and processes your IP address (shortened) and device details on our behalf; Google may process this data outside the UK under the required safeguards. We use this only to improve the site, never for advertising. You can change your mind at any time: change cookie settings.

We also keep a few settings in your browser (local storage): your cookie choice and display choices such as decimal or fractional odds. They never leave your device.

Who hosts the site

GitHub Pages (GitHub) hosts this website and keeps standard server logs. We never sell or share data with anyone, including bookmakers.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, and ask us to correct or delete it. Email tom@betmeisters.com. If you are unhappy with how we handle your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Age

BetMeisters is for adults only (18+). Our tips are opinions, not guarantees. If gambling stops being fun, get free, confidential help from GambleAware.org or the National Gambling Helpline on 0808 8020 133 (UK).
