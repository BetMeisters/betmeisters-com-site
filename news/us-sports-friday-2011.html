Friday night in American sport brings nine NBA games, more than 40 men's college basketball games and six college football games. Every chance below is Pinnacle's price with the margin removed, taken through odds-api.io on Friday evening. Times are UK first (late Friday night and early Saturday morning), with US Eastern Time (ET) in brackets. There is no NHL on our price feed tonight, so hockey is left out.

NBA: nine games from midnight UK (7pm ET)

Atlanta Hawks v Charlotte Hornets (midnight UK, 7pm ET)

Atlanta are 68% to win (fair price 1.46), with a spread of Hawks −7 and a total of 219 points.

Brooklyn Nets v Orlando Magic (12.30am UK, 7.30pm ET)

Orlando are 70% (fair price 1.43) and 6.5-point favourites. The total is 223.5.

Cleveland Cavaliers v Indiana Pacers (12.30am UK, 7.30pm ET)

Cleveland 68%, Cavaliers −6. This game has the second-highest total of the night at 234.5.

Detroit Pistons v Toronto Raptors (12.30am UK, 7.30pm ET)

Detroit are 71% (fair price 1.41), Pistons −7, and the total of 216.5 is the lowest on the NBA card.

Chicago Bulls v Washington Wizards (1am UK, 8pm ET)

One of the closer games: Chicago 56%, Washington 44%, with the Bulls 2.5-point favourites. The total, 238.5, is the highest of the night.

Oklahoma City Thunder v New Orleans Pelicans (1am UK, 8pm ET)

The Thunder are 84% (fair price 1.20) and 12-point favourites, with a total of 228.5.

Utah Jazz v Phoenix Suns (2.30am UK, 9.30pm ET)

Phoenix are 62% on the road, Suns −4.5, total 225.

Los Angeles Lakers v Sacramento Kings (3am UK, 10pm ET)

The biggest favourites in the NBA tonight: the Lakers are 88% (fair price 1.14) and −14.5 on the spread. Sacramento are about 12%.

Denver Nuggets v Houston Rockets (3am UK, 10pm ET)

The closest NBA game of the night. Pinnacle has Denver 52% and Houston 48%, with the Nuggets only 1.5-point favourites. The total is 226.5.

College basketball: the pick of the night

  • Indiana v Kentucky (1am UK, 8pm ET): the headline game. Indiana 60%, Kentucky 40%, Hoosiers −3, total 154.5.
  • Xavier v Iowa (12.30am UK, 7.30pm ET): Xavier 60%, −3.5, total 144.
  • Wisconsin v Murray State (midnight UK, 7pm ET): Wisconsin 61%, −3.5, and a high total of 162.5.
  • Georgia Tech v Georgia (1am UK, 8pm ET): the in-state game has Georgia 78% away, −8.5, total 165.5.
  • Dayton v Utah (10.30pm UK, 5.30pm ET): close to a coin flip, Utah 53%.
  • Saint Mary's v Saint Louis (4am UK Saturday, 11pm ET): another near coin flip, Saint Mary's 53%, total 161.5.
  • Liberty v Rhode Island (9.30pm UK, 4.30pm ET) and Western Kentucky v Richmond (midnight UK): both exactly 50-50 on Pinnacle's money line.

Several of the big programmes play heavy favourites tonight, which shows up as huge spreads rather than money-line prices: Virginia −41.5 v Loyola Maryland, Arkansas −38, Missouri −36 and Maryland −34. Illinois are −49 against Eastern Illinois.

College football: six Friday night games

  • Michigan State v Oregon (1am UK, 8pm ET): Oregon 88% on the road, Ducks −17.5, total 44.
  • Duke v Clemson (12.30am UK, 7.30pm ET): Duke 82%, −12.5, total 40.5.
  • UCF v Iowa State (11pm UK, 6pm ET): the closest game of the night. Iowa State 51%, UCF 49%, total 48.
  • Toledo v Bowling Green (12.30am UK): Toledo 86%, −16.5.
  • Wyoming v New Mexico (2am UK): New Mexico 76% away, −9, and the lowest total of the night at 37.5.
  • Air Force v UTEP (1am UK): Air Force 93%, −22.
