The dry autumn is reshaping the jumps calendar: Ludlow out until 2027, Taunton all-hurdles today

Ludlow has handed 12 fixtures to other courses, Taunton's chases were dropped from today's card and an extra Ffos Las meeting has been added for Sunday. What has moved, and what it means for your bets.

Horse racingUKGeneral news
By the BetMeisters desk26 November 20264 min read

A hot, dry summer and a dry September and October have left several British jumps courses short of good turf. Over the last fortnight the British Horseracing Authority (BHA) has moved, changed or added a run of fixtures. If you bet ante-post or follow particular courses, here is what has changed.

Ludlow: no racing until autumn 2027

Ludlow has transferred its remaining 2026/27 jumps fixtures, and the start of 2027/28, to other courses. The BHA says the turf on Ludlow's free-draining gravel deteriorated faster than it could recover, and Environment Agency limits on how much water the course may take restricted watering. Racing is due to return in autumn 2027.

The 12 fixtures go to: Huntingdon (23 Nov), Catterick (2 Dec), Ayr (16 Dec), Taunton (7 Jan), Warwick (21 Jan), Wincanton (10 and 24 Feb), Musselburgh (4 Mar), Exeter (25 Mar), Fakenham (5 Apr), Market Rasen (20 Apr) and Huntingdon (9 May).

Taunton today: an all-hurdle card

Both steeplechases on today's Taunton card (Thursday 26 November) were abandoned, and an extra Class 4 handicap hurdle (0-115) over three miles was added, so the card is now all hurdles. The BHA's release does not give a reason. Taunton's chief executive described about 150mm of irrigation over six weeks before recent rain, with the ground expected to be on the quick side. Wincanton raced an all-hurdle card on 24 November too.

Extra meetings: Ffos Las on Sunday

The BHA has added a seven-race jumps fixture at Ffos Las on Sunday 29 November. Entries close at noon today and declarations are on Friday morning. An extra Bangor-on-Dee meeting was added on 23 November.

15 December: two meetings moved

Wincanton's fixture moves from 15 to 13 December and Catterick's to 17 December, so people can attend the funeral of former jockey and BHA steward Tom O'Brien at Exeter Cathedral that morning. The races are unchanged, and the evening Flat meeting at Wolverhampton goes ahead.

What it means for your bets

  • Abandoned races: most bookmakers make bets on an abandoned race void and return the stake, but rules differ, especially for ante-post bets. Check your bookmaker's rules for abandoned and rescheduled meetings.
  • Quick ground: horses that need soft ground may be withdrawn late. A late non-runner can trigger a Rule 4 deduction and change the each-way places; our non-runners and Rule 4 guide explains how.
  • Moved fixtures: a race that keeps its conditions but moves course can suit different horses. Form at Ludlow, a sharp track, is not a guide to Ayr or Catterick.
