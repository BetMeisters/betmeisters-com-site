BetMeisters.com is live: every tip free to read until 1 January, and the full record behind it

Our tips site opens to everyone today. +140.28 points from 655 settled tips since 5 November, 15 of 19 days in profit, and the honest parts too: where it works, where it does not, and what happens on 1 January.

FootballTennisIce hockeyHorse racingGeneral newsSports bettingUK
By the BetMeisters desk23 November 20267 min read

BetMeisters.com, the tips site behind the tip boxes in our news, went live today, Monday 23 November 2026. Everything on it is free to read until 1 January, Pro tips included. There is nothing to sign up for and no login.

The idea is simple: let the Meisters prove themselves in public before anyone is asked to pay. Every tip is published before the start, with the price, the stake and the chance we give it, and every result stays on the record afterwards. Nothing is deleted, nothing is re-priced after the event, and losers are counted exactly like winners.

The record in one paragraph

Since 5 November, when the current record began, the Meisters have settled 655 tips for +140.28 points at the advised stakes, a return of +21.4% on everything staked. Over 19 days of results, 15 finished in profit; the best day made +34.65 points and the worst lost 3.79.

The Meisters, one by one

  • Number Cruncher Meister: +126.23 pts from 567 settled tips (275 won). Every tip comes from the numbers. We take the sharp market's price, remove its margin to get a fair chance, and only tip when a bookmaker's price beats that fair chance by a set margin.
  • The Original Meister: +11.05 pts from 112 settled tips (55 won). Picks made by hand: form, the market and judgement. Each one still shows the price and our fair chance, so you can see when it is a value bet and when it is an opinion.
  • NCM Racing Meister: +3.00 pts from 5 settled tips (1 won). Horse racing by numbers, using the betting exchange as the fair price. It started on 19 November, so it is far too early to judge.

Alongside them, the site runs accas and combos, from the Acca Meister and Combo Meister and from The Original Meister's own multiples, each leg with its own price and chance. They have their own pages and their own record, kept separate from the singles above.

Beating the closing price

Profit over three weeks can be luck. A better test is whether a tip's price was better than the price when the market closed, just before the start. Of the 293 settled tips where we have a reliable closing price, 222 (76%) were published at a bigger price than the close, on average +5.4% better. Consistently beating the close is the clearest sign that a method finds value rather than gets lucky.

The honest bits

  • It is early. Under three weeks of results is a small sample. A good run can flatter any tipster, and a bad one can hide a good method. Every result stays on the page either way.
  • Most of the profit is the Number Cruncher's. The Original Meister is in profit but only just, and the racing Meister has too few settled tips to judge.
  • Some sports have not worked. Basketball is slightly down over more than a hundred tips, and darts is down on very few.
  • We have tightened the rules. On 22 November we stopped tipping handball, volleyball and obscure leagues, to stick to the sports and leagues most readers know and can bet on. Old tips in those sports stay on the record.
  • Prices move. Every tip shows the price when it was published. You may get more or less by the time you bet, which changes your own results.

What you will find on BetMeisters.com

  • Today's tips in one place, Free and Pro, with the start time in UK time and in your own local time.
  • Accas and combos, every leg priced, with the chance that the whole multiple lands.
  • Results and performance: every settled tip, the running profit, and the record for each Meister and each sport.

What happens on 1 January

Until 1 January every tip on the site is free to read. From 1 January the Pro tips will become part of a paid membership; Free tips and the full public record stay open to everyone.

For people who follow from the start, there will be a founders' offer: a special deal for early members, announced on the site and on our social channels before 1 January. We are not announcing prices today. The point of the next five weeks is for you to judge the tips first.

BetMeisters.com takes no affiliate deals, adverts or sponsorship, so no tip is there to send you to a bookmaker. The tips on BetMeisters.news are copied from it exactly as published, and every tip box here links straight to it.
