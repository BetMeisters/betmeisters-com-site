Sunday's racing: two Grade 1s at Fairyhouse, the Hatton's Grace and the Drinmore, plus three British meetings

Fairyhouse's big Sunday leads four meetings and 28 races: the Hatton's Grace Hurdle, the Drinmore Novice Chase, the Royal Bond and a 16-runner Porterstown handicap. Who is fresh, who is first time out this season, and the each-way terms.

Sunday's racing is led by Fairyhouse, with two Grade 1 races, a Grade 2 and two Listed handicaps on an eight-race card. In Britain there are jumps meetings at Carlisle, Ffos Las and Leicester: four meetings and 28 races in all. Our NCM Racing Meister tips appear on the horse racing tips page when a price beats the exchanges' fair chance.

The meetings

  • Fairyhouse (Ireland): 8 races from 12pm to 3.41pm. Going: hurdles yielding, chases good to yielding.
  • Carlisle (jumps): 7 races from 12.10pm.
  • Ffos Las (jumps): 7 races from 12.20pm.
  • Leicester (jumps): 6 races from 12.50pm.

The going for the three British meetings was not in the racecard data when we checked; check each course's latest update.

Fairyhouse 2.30 · Hatton's Grace Hurdle (Grade 1), 2m4f, 6 runners (€72,000)

The best race of the day. Teahupoo (Gordon Elliott, Jack Kennedy) is the highest rated at 161 and returns after 213 days off. Jimmy Du Seuil (Willie Mullins, Paul Townend, 156) is also first time out this season, 213 days since his last run. Wodhooh (Elliott, 154), a mare with form figures 1-1-1-3, has been off for 211 days. Two have already run this season: Casheldale Lad (Elliott, 150; third 15 days ago) and Blake (Noel Meade, 139; second eight days ago). Gordon Elliott trains four of the six.

Fairyhouse 2.00 · Drinmore Novice Chase (Grade 1), 2m4f, 9 runners (€54,000)

A deep novice chase. King Alexander (Mullins, Townend) is the highest rated at 148 and was sixth last time out, 67 days ago. Come Walk With Me (Edward Cawley, 146) ended last season with two wins and is back after 213 days. Shadow Paddy (142) has form figures 1-1-1-4. Gordon Elliott runs three: Ballyfad, Bowensonfire (a winner last time, 137) and Kazansky. Love Me Tender (Mullins, Harry Cobden) has won her last two.

Each way: eight or more runners in a non-handicap: standard terms three places at 1/5. One non-runner would make it two places at 1/4.

Fairyhouse 1.30 · Royal Bond Novice Hurdle (Grade 2), 2m, 7 runners (€51,000)

Skerry Hill (Elliott, Kennedy) is unbeaten in the form shown (1/11-1) and won 29 days ago. Magnifico Conti (Mullins, Townend) won his only listed run and returns after 279 days. Huntin Boots (Elliott) won the day before, so is turned out quickly. Switch From Diesel (127) and Mr Hollywood (124) are the highest rated.

Each way: two places at 1/4.

Fairyhouse 1.00 · Porterstown Handicap Chase (Listed), 3m5f, 16 runners (€27,000)

A marathon handicap. Better Days Ahead (Elliott) and Quai De Bourbon (Mullins) top the weights on 152; Favori De Champdou (150) returns after 232 days. Gordon Elliott runs seven of the 16. Bossofthebrownies (139, second 25 days ago) is among the more recently raced.

Each way: a handicap with 16 or more runners usually pays four places at 1/5; a single non-runner would cut that to three places at 1/4. Our each-way guide shows why that matters.

Fairyhouse 3.07 · Listed handicap hurdle, 2m, 15 runners (€60,000)

The richest handicap of the day. Helvic Dream (Meade, 142) won last time out; Lazare de Star (Elliott, 132) is back after 238 days. Sea Eagle and Firebrand come in on recent form.

Each way: 12 to 15 runners in a handicap: standard terms three places at 1/4.

Carlisle 2.10 · Handicap chase, 3m1f, 10 runners (£9,506)

The best of the British racing. Famous Bridge (Nicky Richards, 127) tops the weights after 225 days off; Just Golden (Dan Skelton, 123) was second 33 days ago; Hello Judge (Ann Hamilton, 122) won last time out, 20 days ago.

Each way: 8 to 11 runners: three places at 1/5.

How to use this card

The ratings are official handicap marks; form figures read left to right with the most recent run last (P = pulled up, F = fell, U = unseated). Horses off for 200 days or more are first time out this season, which can go either way. For how we price runners, see our horse racing tips page.
