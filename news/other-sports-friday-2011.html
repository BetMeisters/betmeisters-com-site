Friday night: Alcaraz and Medvedev in Turin, Olympiacos v Real Madrid and a nine-game NBA slate

The last group day at the ATP Finals, four Euroleague games, the Grand Slam of Darts quarter-finals and a full NBA and college basketball night. Every main event with its chance on the sharp market.

Friday away from football starts in Turin, where the ATP Finals round-robin finishes with Carlos Alcaraz v Flavio Cobolli at 1pm UK and Daniil Medvedev v Frances Tiafoe in the evening. Euroleague basketball has four games from 5.30pm, the Grand Slam of Darts reaches the quarter-finals in Wolverhampton, and the NBA has nine games overnight.

Every chance below comes from Pinnacle, the sharpest bookmaker in the market, with its margin removed, from BetMeisters' scan at 11am UK. They show what the market thinks, not what will happen. Where we give a handicap or a total, it is the line the market prices closest to 50/50. Times are UK, with US Eastern in brackets for the late games.

Tennis: ATP Finals, Turin

  • Carlos Alcaraz v Flavio Cobolli (1pm): Alcaraz is a big favourite at 82.0%.
  • Simone Bolelli / Andrea Vavassori v Kevin Krawietz / Tim Pütz (5pm, doubles): the Germans are 58.4%.
  • Daniil Medvedev v Frances Tiafoe (7.30pm): Medvedev 68.1%, Tiafoe 31.9%.

Euroleague

  • Anadolu Efes v ASVEL (5.30pm): Efes 73.0% at home; Efes −8.5 is the even line and the total about 176.5.
  • Olympiacos v Real Madrid (7.15pm): the game of the night and close to a coin flip, Olympiacos 53.4%, Real Madrid 46.6%. Olympiacos −1.5 is the even line and the total about 174.5.
  • Barcelona v Crvena Zvezda (7.30pm): Barcelona 57.9%, with Barcelona −2.5 the even handicap.
  • Partizan v Besiktas (7.45pm): Partizan 67.5%, Partizan −5.5 the even line, total about 164.5.

All four home sides are favourites, but only Efes by more than a basket or two on the even handicap.

Darts: Grand Slam of Darts, Wolverhampton

Jermaine Wattimena v Kevin Doets (7.15pm): the market makes Doets the favourite, 59.5%, with Wattimena 40.5%.

NBA

  • Atlanta v Charlotte (midnight UK, 7pm ET): Atlanta 66.2%, Atlanta −5.5 the even line, total about 220.
  • Brooklyn v Orlando (12.30am UK): Orlando 67.4% away, Brooklyn +5.5 the even line.
  • Cleveland v Indiana (12.30am UK): Cleveland 72.0%, Indiana +7.5 the even line, total about 234.
  • Detroit v Toronto (12.30am UK): Detroit 72.2%, Toronto +7.5, total about 218.
  • Chicago v Washington (1am UK): Chicago 55.2%, and the highest total of the night at about 241.
  • Oklahoma City v New Orleans (1am UK): Oklahoma City 83.6%, OKC −11.5 the even line.
  • Utah v Phoenix (2.30am UK): Phoenix 62.8% away, Phoenix −4.5.
  • LA Lakers v Sacramento (3am UK): the biggest favourite of the night, Lakers 87.7%, Sacramento +14.5.
  • Denver v Houston (3am UK): the closest game, Denver 51.4%, Houston +1.5 the even line, total about 225.

College basketball

A busy NCAA night. The biggest game is Indiana v Kentucky (1am UK, 8pm ET), where Indiana are 57.7% at home with Indiana −3 the even line. Xavier are 60.2% against Iowa (12.30am), Georgia 78.1% at Georgia Tech (1am) and Saint Mary's 55.2% against Saint Louis (4am).

How to read these numbers

A chance is not a tip. A 72% favourite still loses about one game in four, and a coin-flip game is exactly that. The numbers are useful because they let you compare a price with the market: if a bookmaker offers more than the fair price for a side (for 50%, that is anything above 2.00), the price is better than the market thinks it should be.
