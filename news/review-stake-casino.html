Our view in one minute

Stake is one of the biggest crypto-first gambling brands in the world, and its terms are clearer than many offshore sites on the basics: player money is held in separate customer accounts, and complaints can go free of charge to CADRE, an independent dispute body certified by the Curaçao regulator. But the Curaçao register shows its licence period ended on 9 June 2026 and is listed as "Assessment in progress": the renewal is under review and not yet decided. You must also wager every deposit in full before you can withdraw it. This review covers Stake's casino; see also our Stake sportsbook review.

Licence and where it applies

Licence: Curaçao Gaming Authority, licence OGL/2024/1451/0918, held by Medium Rare N.V. (company 145353), Willemstad, Curaçao. Register of 20 November 2026: issued 9 December 2025, licence period ended 9 June 2026, status "Assessment in progress". Some payments are handled by Stake's subsidiaries in Cyprus.

Where you cannot play: Stake lists prohibited jurisdictions including the United Kingdom, the United States, Australia, France, Germany, Spain, Italy, the Netherlands, Ireland, Brazil, Mexico, South Africa and Ontario and Alberta in Canada, among others (14.3). Using a VPN or false details to get round this is a breach of its terms (14.4).

Payments and withdrawals

  • Play-through: you must wager 100% of each deposit before requesting a withdrawal, crypto or fiat (8.5, 8.10).
  • Fees: Stake may charge a withdrawal fee at its discretion, and payment providers may add their own (8.9, 7.8).
  • Same method: withdrawals go back through the method you deposited with, split across methods if you used several (8.3).
  • Checks: Stake can carry out extra identity checks on any withdrawal and delay or decline payments while it investigates, without explaining why (8.2, 8.12).
  • Your money: deposits and winnings are held in separate customer bank accounts or crypto wallets (9.1).

The casino

Stake's terms say it can adjust the house edge of any slot game at its sole discretion and without notice, and that if the house edge shown differs from a game's stated return to player (RTP) you should ask support; it accepts no liability if you bet relying on the difference (10.5). That is unusually blunt: check a game's information panel before you play, and treat RTP as a long-run average, not a price.

Terms: the good and the bad

  • Good: a free, independent complaints route (CADRE) after Stake's own process, with Stake committing to acknowledge a complaint within a week (23.1-23.3).
  • Good: two-factor sign-in is recommended and available (4.17).
  • Bad: broad rights to void bets and keep winnings if Stake considers use "unfair" or "improper" (4.19-4.21), and the right to change the terms without notice (3.4).
  • Bad: unclaimed bonuses can be cancelled after 60 days or sooner, and any bonus can be cancelled at Stake's discretion (12.10, 12.11).

FAQs

Can I use Stake from the UK? No. The UK is a prohibited jurisdiction and the Curaçao licence does not cover UK players.

Is Stake's licence valid? The Curaçao register of 20 November 2026 lists it as "Assessment in progress": the licence period ended on 9 June 2026 and the renewal had not been decided at that date.

Can I complain to someone independent? Yes: after Stake's final decision, to CADRE B.V., free of charge.
