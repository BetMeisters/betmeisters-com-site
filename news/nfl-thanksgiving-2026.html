NFL Thanksgiving 2026: Bears at Lions, Eagles at Cowboys and Chiefs at Bills, plus Packers at Rams the night before

Four games in two days, with UK and US times, where to watch in the US, and the sharp market's spreads and totals for each one, margin removed.

The NFL's Thanksgiving week now starts a day early. A game on Thanksgiving Eve is followed by the traditional three on Thursday 26 November, and a Black Friday game after that. Fixtures and TV are from the NFL's schedule announcement; the lines are from Pinnacle's prices with the margin removed, taken on Tuesday 24 November.

The games

  • Packers at Rams, Wednesday 25 November, 8pm Eastern (1am Thursday UK), Netflix.
  • Bears at Lions, Thursday, 1pm Eastern (6pm UK), CBS.
  • Eagles at Cowboys, Thursday, 4.30pm Eastern (9.30pm UK), FOX.
  • Chiefs at Bills, Thursday, 8.20pm Eastern (1.20am Friday UK), NBC.
  • Black Friday: Broncos at Steelers, 3pm Eastern (8pm UK), Prime Video.

What the market says

  • Packers at Rams: the Rams are about 2-point favourites (Rams −2 is a 50% chance), and the total is about 44 points.
  • Bears at Lions: the Bears are favoured on the road by about 2.5 points, and the total is the highest of the week at about 55.
  • Eagles at Cowboys: the biggest spread of the week. Dallas are 8.5-point favourites (Cowboys −8.5 is a 50% chance), with a total of about 48.
  • Chiefs at Bills: Buffalo are 2.5-point favourites (52%), total about 47.

These are the lines where each side is close to a 50% chance, which is how the sharp market sets a spread. A spread of 3 or less, as in three of these games, means the market sees the game as close to even, with a small allowance for home field.

Things to know before you bet

  • Short weeks. Every Thursday team played on Sunday, so injury reports and late inactives matter more than usual. Lines can move on Wednesday's and Thursday's reports.
  • Overtime counts. Spreads, totals and money lines are settled including overtime at most bookmakers. Whole-number spreads can push.
  • UK times. Two of the five games finish after 4am UK. Set a limit before you start, not after midnight.

For how spreads, totals and money lines work, see our guide to betting odds. BetMeisters has no NFL tips for these games yet. Any will appear on the tips page with the price, the chance and the edge.
