From Monastir to Bonn: the Acca Meister's 5-fold across tennis, CS2 and European basketball at 121.72

Five legs in three sports, from an ITF match at 12.30 Kampala time to the FIBA Champions League in the evening. Long odds, and it lands about 1 time in 93.

The Acca Meister's other-sports acca for Tuesday has five legs in three sports, from a lunchtime tennis match in Tunisia to three basketball handicaps in the evening. Combined, it is a 5-fold at 121.72. Every leg was priced above its fair chance on the sharp market when it was built.

The five legs

  • 12:30 · Samir Hamza Reguig v Melvin Kumar (ITF Men Monastir 2 R1): Melvin Kumar to win at 3.95. Fair chance about 26.4%.
  • 15:00 · Hype Tech v ex-INOX (CS2 NODWIN Clutch Series): ex-INOX to win at 2.85. Fair chance about 36.7%.
  • 20:00 · Elitzur Yavne v AS Ramat Hasharon (Israel National League): Elitzur Yavne +9.5 at 1.73. Fair chance about 60.6%.
  • 20:00 · Balkan Botevgrad v Ulm Basketball (Eurocup): Balkan Botevgrad +2 at 2.5. Fair chance about 41.8%.
  • 20:30 · Bnei Herzliya Basket v Baskets Bonn (FIBA Champions League): Bnei Herzliya +5 at 2.5. Fair chance about 43.6%.

What each leg needs

Melvin Kumar to win his first-round match at the ITF event in Monastir. He is the underdog, at about a 26% chance, so this is the leg most likely to sink the acca early. If either player retires, the bookmaker's own tennis rules decide how the leg settles, and they differ between firms.

ex-INOX to win against Hype Tech in the CS2 NODWIN Clutch Series, another underdog leg at about 37%.

The three basketball legs are all handicaps on the underdog: Elitzur Yavne +9.5 (they must not lose by 10 or more), Balkan Botevgrad +2 in the Eurocup (a win or a one-point defeat wins the leg, a defeat by exactly two returns its stake, and a defeat by three or more loses), and Bnei Herzliya +5 in the FIBA Champions League (a win or a defeat by four or fewer wins the leg; a five-point defeat returns its stake). Overtime counts in basketball handicaps.

The honest numbers

The five fair chances multiply to about 1.1%: roughly 1 in 93. At 121.72 that is a paper edge of about +30%, but with two underdog legs before the evening even starts, this acca will lose far more often than it wins. When it was built, none of the five prices was matched by a second bookmaker or the exchange, so they may not last. The Acca Meister stakes it at 0.5 points.

How to think about accas

  • Small stakes only. Long-odds accas go long runs without landing.
  • Each leg should be value on its own. A leg priced below its fair chance drags the whole acca down.
  • Never chase. A losing acca is the normal result, not a near miss.
