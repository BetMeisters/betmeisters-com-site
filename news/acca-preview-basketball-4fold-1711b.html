Hong Kong to the Eurocup: the Acca Meister's basketball 4-fold at 7.98

Three short-priced favourites and one Eurocup underdog with a start. It lands about 1 time in 6.

The Acca Meister's Tuesday basketball 4-fold starts in Hong Kong at 15:40 Kampala time and finishes with a Eurocup game in the evening, a 4-fold at 7.98. Three legs are favourites to win outright; the fourth is the value leg, Balkan Botevgrad +1.5 at home to Ulm, where the sharp market makes Ulm about 68% to win the game.

The 4 legs

  • 15:40 · Hong Kong Eastern Long Lions v Shung Tak Alumni Eagle (Hong Kong): Hong Kong Eastern Long Lions to win at 1.5. Fair chance about 70.7%. What it needs: a straight win (overtime counts in basketball).
  • 19:30 · Maccabi Raanana v BC Otef Darom (Israel National League): Maccabi Raanana to win at 1.5. Fair chance about 70.4%. What it needs: a straight win (overtime counts in basketball).
  • 20:00 · BC Jurbarkas-Karys v Gargzdai Basketball (Lithuania King Mindaugas Cup): Gargzdai Basketball to win at 1.364. Fair chance about 75.8%. What it needs: a straight win (overtime counts in basketball).
  • 20:00 · Balkan Botevgrad v Ulm Basketball (Eurocup): Balkan Botevgrad +1.5 at 2.6. Fair chance about 41.3%. What it needs: Balkan Botevgrad cover unless they lose by 2 or more; overtime counts.

The honest numbers

Multiply the fair chances together and the acca lands about 15.5% of the time: roughly 1 in 6. At 7.98 that is an edge of about +24% on paper. Edge is a long-run idea; over one bet the most likely result is that it loses. The stake is 0.5 points.

Before you copy it

  • Small stakes only. Multiples go long runs without landing.
  • Prices move. If a leg has shortened, the whole price drops with it.
  • Never chase. A losing multiple is the normal result, not a near miss.
