Paraguay, US college and a Lithuanian upset: the Acca Meister's basketball 5-fold at 45.5

Four overnight handicaps across South America and US college basketball, then Nevezis to beat Rytas on Wednesday. It lands about 1 time in 31.

This 5-fold at 45.5 runs from 02:00 Kampala time on Wednesday, with a Paraguayan league game, two US college games and a game in Argentina's second tier, and finishes on Wednesday evening in Lithuania, where the long leg is Nevezis to beat Rytas at 3.50. Two legs, Hindu Club +9.5 and Nevezis, are also in an earlier Acca Meister acca.

The 5 legs

  • 02:00 · Deportivo San Jose v Olimpia Kings (Paraguay LNB): Deportivo San Jose -3.5 at 1.8. Fair chance about 60.4%. What it needs: Deportivo San Jose must win by 4 points or more; overtime counts.
  • 03:00 · Florida State Seminoles v Ball State Cardinals (USA NCAA): Florida State -25.5 at 2. Fair chance about 55.0%. What it needs: Florida State must win by 26 points or more; overtime counts.
  • 03:00 · Santa Paula de Galvez v Hindu Club (Argentina Liga Argentina): Hindu Club Cordoba +9.5 at 1.833. Fair chance about 58.9%. What it needs: Hindu Club Cordoba cover unless they lose by 10 or more; overtime counts.
  • 03:30 · Louisiana Tech Bulldogs v Jacksonville State Gamecocks (USA NCAA): Louisiana Tech -1.5 at 1.97. Fair chance about 53.8%. What it needs: Louisiana Tech must win by 2 points or more; overtime counts.
  • 19:50 · BC Nevezis Kedainiai v BC Rytas Vilnius (Lithuania LKL): BC Nevezis Kedainiai to win at 3.5. Fair chance about 30.7%. What it needs: a straight win (overtime counts in basketball).

The honest numbers

Multiply the fair chances together and the acca lands about 3.2% of the time: roughly 1 in 31, ignoring half-wins and returned stakes. At 45.5 that is an edge of about +47% on paper. Edge is a long-run idea; over one bet the most likely result is that it loses. The stake is 0.5 points.

Before you copy it

  • Small stakes only. Multiples go long runs without landing.
  • Prices move. If a leg has shortened, the whole price drops with it.
  • Never chase. A losing multiple is the normal result, not a near miss.
