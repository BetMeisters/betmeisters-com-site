Meister previews · Thursday 26 November

Thursday's racing: Gerri Colombe heads a Listed chase at Thurles and Kala Conti goes for a Listed mares' prize at Market Rasen

Four jumps meetings in the afternoon and an evening on the all-weather at Chelmsford, where a £20,936 novice race is the best Flat prize. Every meeting, its going and the races to watch.

Thursday's racing runs from 11.54am to 8.30pm UK time across five meetings and 35 races: four jumps meetings in the afternoon, at Market Rasen, Taunton, Lingfield and Thurles in Ireland, and then the all-weather at Chelmsford into the evening.

The meetings

  • Market Rasen (jumps, good to soft): 7 races from 11.54am to 3.24pm, with a Listed mares' chase at 1.04pm (£23,092), the richest race of the day.
  • Thurles (Ireland, jumps, good): 7 races from 12.11pm to 3.39pm, with a Listed chase at 12.46pm (€15,601).
  • Taunton (jumps, good to firm): 6 races from 12.20pm to 3.15pm.
  • Lingfield (jumps, good): 6 races from 12.37pm to 3.32pm.
  • Chelmsford (all-weather, standard): 9 races from 4.30pm to 8.30pm, with a Class 2 novice race at 7pm (£20,936).

Thurles 12.46pm · Listed chase, 2m6f, 7 runners (€15,601)

A strong field for a midweek Listed race, and most of them are having their first run of the season. Gerri Colombe (Gordon Elliott, Jack Kennedy) is the highest rated on 160 but fell last time and has been off for 229 days. Henry de Bromhead's Monty's Star (157) is also back after 229 days. Willie Mullins runs four: Ile Atlantique (155), Lecky Watson (154), Champ Kiely (152) and High Class Hero (149), with Paul Townend on Champ Kiely. Barry Connell's Apple's Of Bresil (128) is the only one with a recent run, third 43 days ago. Horses returning from long breaks are hard to price, so expect the market to move once the yards' intentions are clearer.

Market Rasen 1.04pm · Listed mares' chase, 2m7f191y, 5 runners (£23,092)

Gordon Elliott sends Kala Conti (Danny Gilligan) over from Ireland. She is rated 148, at least 12lb clear of her rivals on official ratings, and won 26 days ago. Bluey (Emma Lavelle, Harry Cobden) and Ruby Island (Nicky Richards, Danny McMenamin) are both rated 136 and ran within the last three weeks; Ruby Island won last time. Jasmine Bliss (Harry Derham, 133) and Overabottleofred (Tom Gretton, Sean Bowen, 115) both return after more than 220 days.

Chelmsford 7pm · Class 2 novice stakes, 7f, 6 runners (£20,936)

The best Flat prize of the day for lightly raced two-year-olds. Ed Walker's Space Bob (Kieran Shoemark) was second on his only start. Charlie Appleby's Force of Light (Billy Loughnane) and Andrew Balding's Nostalgic Warrior (P. J. McDonald) were both fifth on debut, and Balding also runs the newcomer Clear Code. Charlie Johnston's Sexy is another debutant.

Taunton 12.20pm and Lingfield 2.22pm

The Taunton opener is a qualifier for a £50,000 Cheltenham bonus, with Dan Skelton's Fortis Et Liber (rated 112) the only one with an official rating. At Lingfield, the novices' limited handicap chase brings together Blue Carpet (Dan Skelton, Harry Skelton) and Fortune Timmy (Chris Gordon), both rated 132 and both back after more than 220 days, with Nicky Henderson's Laughing John (122) running 30 days after a fall.
