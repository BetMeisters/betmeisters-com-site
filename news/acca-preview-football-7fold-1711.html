Kyrgyzstan to Rushden: the Acca Meister's 7-fold at 192

A friendly, two AFCON qualifiers and four English non-league handicaps, staked at 0.25 points. It lands about 1 time in 128.

The Acca Meister's second long football acca of the day pays 192 from seven legs, staked at 0.25 points. It shares the Botswana draw and Cape Verde −1.25 with the 5-fold and adds four Asian-handicap legs in English non-league football at 7.45pm UK time, where small handicaps on each side let a draw or a narrow result still pay.

The 7 legs

  • 15:00 · Kyrgyzstan v Vietnam (International Matches): Kyrgyzstan +0.5 at 1.6. Fair chance about 65.5%. What it needs: a win or a draw for Kyrgyzstan wins the leg; a defeat loses.
  • 22:00 · Botswana v Uganda (Africa Cup of Nations): Draw at 2.8. Fair chance about 37.6%. What it needs: only a draw will do.
  • 22:00 · Liberia v Cape Verde (Africa Cup of Nations): Cape Verde -1.25 at 3.3. Fair chance about 31.2%. What it needs: Cape Verde must win by 2 goals or more for a full win; a 1-goal win loses half, the other half comes back; a draw or defeat loses.
  • 22:45 · Boldmere St Michaels FC v Loughborough Students (England Amateur Northern Premier League): Loughborough Students +0 (draw no bet) at 1.95. Fair chance about 57.4%. What it needs: Loughborough Students must win; a draw returns the stake; a defeat loses.
  • 22:45 · Guiseley FC v Ashton United FC (England Amateur Northern Premier League): Ashton United +1.25 at 1.8. Fair chance about 59.1%. What it needs: a win or a draw for Ashton United wins the leg; a 1-goal defeat wins half, the other half comes back; a 2-goal defeat or worse loses.
  • 22:45 · Ramsgate FC v Cray Wanderers (England Amateur Isthmian League): Cray Wanderers +0.25 at 1.85. Fair chance about 57.4%. What it needs: Cray Wanderers must win; a draw wins half, the other half comes back; a defeat loses.
  • 22:45 · AFC Rushden & Diamonds v Mickleover FC (England Amateur Northern Premier League): Rushden and Diamonds -0.25 at 2. Fair chance about 52.0%. What it needs: Rushden and Diamonds must win; a draw loses half, the other half comes back; a defeat loses.

The honest numbers

Multiply the fair chances together and the acca lands about 0.8% of the time: roughly 1 in 128, ignoring half-wins and returned stakes. At 192 that is an edge of about +50% on paper. Edge is a long-run idea; over one bet the most likely result is that it loses. The stake is 0.25 points.

Before you copy it

  • Small stakes only. Multiples go long runs without landing.
  • Prices move. If a leg has shortened, the whole price drops with it.
  • Never chase. A losing multiple is the normal result, not a near miss.
