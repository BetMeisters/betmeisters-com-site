Friday's racing: Ascot's jumps card, Chepstow and Catterick, then Wolverhampton and Dundalk under lights

Five meetings and 39 races. Ascot's seven-race jumps card carries the best prize money, with Nicky Henderson's Crystal Island a short favourite in the opener, and the all-weather takes over from 4.30pm. Every meeting, the early market and what to watch.

Friday's racing in Britain and Ireland runs from 11.20am to 8.30pm UK time (6.20am to 3.30pm Eastern) across five meetings and 39 races. Three are over jumps in the daytime: Chepstow (eight races from 11.20am), Catterick (seven from 12pm, good ground) and Ascot (seven from 12.10pm, good ground). In the evening the action moves to the all-weather at Wolverhampton (nine races, 4.30pm to 8.30pm) and Dundalk in Ireland (eight, 4.45pm to 8.15pm).

On Thursday afternoon only Ascot had been priced, and only by a single bookmaker, so the chances below are that bookmaker's early prices with its margin removed (its book on these races runs from 108% to 126%). The exchanges had not formed a market on any of Friday's races, so treat these as a first view, not a verdict. Runners, riders, ratings and form are from The Racing API racecards.

Updated 10:10 UK: non-runners, going and the morning market

Ascot 12.10 is now a walkover. Both Crystal Island, last night's 8/15 favourite, and Gee Force Flyer are non-runners, leaving Joe Tizzard's Etna Bianco as the only declared runner.

Other non-runners: Prometeo (Ascot 1.20, now 5 runners), Gwen's Girl (Catterick 12.00), Jet To London and Moments Away (Catterick 12.33, now 7), Sunset Hill (Catterick 1.43), Auburn Avenue (Chepstow 12.22), Arctic Conditions and Annie Express (Chepstow 2.07), Snowy Hill (Chepstow 3.17), Valor's Call, Scarlett's Spell, Passerine and Dapper Guest (Wolverhampton 5.00, 6.00, 7.00 and 7.30).

Going: Ascot is good, good to firm in places (GoingStick 6.1 on the chase course and 6.7 on the hurdles at 7am, per the course's TurfTrax readings), and the 12.10 is now a walkover, as Ascot has confirmed. Catterick is good. Chepstow's card now lists its hurdle races as soft and its chases as good. Wolverhampton and Dundalk are standard.

The morning market (the median price across about 25 bookmakers this morning, against a single early bookmaker last night):

  • Ascot 12.45: No Way Jay is now a clear evens favourite; Current Edition has drifted to about 6/4. They were joint 11/8 last night.
  • Ascot 1.20: the market has turned round. Spanish Stroll is now favourite at about 5/4, with Push The Limit (last night's 6/5 favourite) about 13/8.
  • Ascot 1.55 (the day's most valuable race in Britain): Western Knight is now favourite at about 2/1, just ahead of Jet To Vegas at about 9/4 (last night's favourite) and Crebilly at 4/1.
  • Ascot 2.30: Wondering Why (Ben Pauling) now heads the betting at about 3/1, with Moveit Like Minnie about 7/2.
  • Ascot 3.05: Draco Malfoy (Kim Bailey and Mat Nicholls) is favourite at about 7/2.
  • Ascot 3.40: Power Steering has shortened to about 4/5.
  • Chepstow 12.57 (the day's best race there): Ben Solo is about 3/1, with Wilstar and Largy Go both about 4/1.
  • Catterick 12.00: Dan Skelton's Sacre Chef is a short favourite at about 8/15.
  • Wolverhampton 7.30 (Class 2 handicap): Asuka (George Boughey, Billy Loughnane) is about 13/8.

Prices are from The Racing API's racecards at about 10:10 UK. They move all morning; check before you bet.

Ascot: the day's best card

12.10 · Beginners' chase, 2m5f (3 runners, Class 2, £10,562)

Only three go to post, and Nicky Henderson's Crystal Island (Nico de Boinville, rated 133, form 1/11-) is the clear favourite at 8/15 on his first run for 213 days. With the margin removed that is about 61%. Olly Murphy's Gee Force Flyer (125, form 32231-) is about 21% and Joe Tizzard's Etna Bianco (126) about 19%. It is a beginners' chase, so none of them has won over fences yet, and all three return from breaks of more than 200 days.

12.45 · Maiden hurdle, 2m3f (8 runners, Class 3, £13,615)

A two-horse market: Tizzard's No Way Jay (Freddie Gingell, second last time 20 days ago) and the Twiston-Davies yard's Current Edition (rated 119, form 532-22) are both 11/8, about 35% each. Current Edition has finished second in each of his last three runs.

1.20 · Introductory hurdle, 1m7f (6 runners, Class 2, £15,843)

A race for horses starting out over hurdles. Dan Skelton's Push The Limit (Tristan Durrell), third on his only run 29 days ago, is 6/5, about 40%. Tizzard's Spanish Stroll (form 90-1) is about 27% and the O'Neills' Without Burlington, a winner on his only start, about 17% on his return from 121 days off.

1.55 · Handicap chase, 2m5f (6 runners, Class 2, £18,210)

The most valuable race of the day in Britain, and a competitive one: the top four are all rated 135 or 136. Jet To Vegas (Lucinda Russell and Michael Scudamore, Derek Fox, form 86P-12) is the early favourite at 9/4, about 27%. Tizzard's Western Knight (about 22%) returns from 253 days off; the O'Neills' Crebilly (form 142131) and Jedd O'Keeffe's Fringill Dike (form -61221) are in-form handicappers at about 20% and 17%.

2.30 · Handicap hurdle, 2m (8 runners, Class 3, £15,947)

Wide open: no runner is better than about 18%. Moveit Like Minnie (Twiston-Davies, 7lb claimer Amber Jackson-Fennell) heads the betting at 7/2, from Skelton's Gambino, Paul Nicholls' Fasol (who ran six days ago) and Daisy Hitchins' top-rated Intellotto (128, won last time).

3.05 and 3.40 · A big-field chase and a bumper

The 3.05 is a ten-runner handicap chase over nearly three miles with no favourite shorter than 11/2: the O'Neills' Yes Day (form 324131, ran nine days ago) heads it. The card closes with a bumper in which Harry Fry's Power Steering, second on his only run, is about 31%.

Chepstow and Catterick

Neither card had been priced when we wrote this, so here are the ratings and form. At Chepstow, the most valuable race is the 12.57 handicap chase (Class 3, 11 runners), where Authentic Legacy (the Moores) and Rebecca Curtis' Ben Solo top the weights on 126, with Kim Bailey and Mat Nicholls' Sogna In Grande (125) and Olly Murphy's Wilstar (125, form /8211-) just below. Two divisions of a novices' hurdle (11.50 and 12.22) and a maiden hurdle at 11.20 give a first look at a few jumpers for the winter; Nicholls' Exciting Ride and Kim Bailey's Hidden Mountain (form 21-1) are among the runners.

Catterick's best race is the 2.18, a three-mile Class 3 handicap chase for horses who came from point-to-points, with Richard Hobson's Some Scope top-rated on 119. The 12.33 novices' hurdle includes the O'Neills' Nightflyer (rated 112, form -52122) and Gary Hanmer's I've Got A Friend (form U2-112). The 2.53 is a handicap chase for female jockeys.

Wolverhampton: the All-Weather Championships handicap

The evening's feature is the 7.30 All-Weather Championship Handicap over seven furlongs (Class 2, £12,988, 10 runners). Ian Williams' Dandy Style (Clifford Lee) tops the ratings on 101 with form 264231 but has been off for 155 days. George Boughey's Asuka (Billy Loughnane, 97, form 211341) ran 22 days ago. Before that, two divisions of a fillies' maiden (5.30 and 6pm) bring out runners from Ralph Beckett (Lady Phillips, second in both her starts) and the Gosdens (Zhuri, rated 75).

Dundalk: eight races under lights

The best prize money at Dundalk is in the two Irish Stallion Farms EBF maidens: the 6.45 for colts and geldings and the 7.15 for fillies (€13,200 each). The fillies' race has several unraced runners from big yards, including Henry de Bromhead's Always Be Mine, Jim Bolger's An Tuath Nua and Joseph O'Brien's Arddin. The 6.15 rated race (€12,000) has Joseph O'Brien's Celtic Motif top-rated on 88, with Denis Hogan's Apache Outlaw (84, form 396951, Colin Keane) next.
