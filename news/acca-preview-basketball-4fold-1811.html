Monmouth +15.5, Rider +43.5 and an upset in Lithuania: the Acca Meister's basketball 4-fold at 23.02

Three big underdog handicaps overnight in Argentina and US college basketball, then Nevezis to beat Rytas in Lithuania on Wednesday. It lands about 1 time in 18.

The Acca Meister's acca for Wednesday is all basketball: three handicaps on underdogs that tip off at 3am Kampala time, then a straight upset pick in Lithuania on Wednesday evening. Combined, it is a 4-fold at 23.02.

The four legs

  • 03:00 · Santa Paula de Galvez v Hindu Club (Argentina Liga Argentina): Hindu Club Cordoba +9.5 at 1.833. Fair chance about 58.9%.
  • 03:00 · Wake Forest Demon Deacons v Monmouth Hawks (USA NCAA): Monmouth +15.5 at 1.909. Fair chance about 56.1%.
  • 03:00 · Miami Hurricanes v Rider Broncs (USA NCAA): Rider +43.5 at 1.88. Fair chance about 56.1%.
  • 19:50 · BC Nevezis Kedainiai v BC Rytas Vilnius (Lithuania LKL): BC Nevezis Kedainiai to win at 3.5. Fair chance about 30.7%.

What each leg needs

Hindu Club +9.5 away to Santa Paula de Galvez in Argentina's Liga Argentina: they must not lose by 10 or more.

Monmouth +15.5 at Wake Forest and Rider +43.5 at Miami are early-season US college games, where big programmes host much smaller schools. The spreads are huge, but the market still prices each close to 50/50; the Acca Meister found both slightly above their fair chance. Each underdog covers if it loses by less than the number (15 or fewer for Monmouth, 43 or fewer for Rider). Overtime counts.

BC Nevezis Kedainiai to win at home to BC Rytas Vilnius in the Lithuanian league on Wednesday evening, at about a 31% chance. This is the long leg, and if the first three land it will decide the acca.

The honest numbers

The four fair chances multiply to about 5.7%: roughly 1 in 18. At 23.02 that is a paper edge of about +31%. Over one bet the most likely result is that it loses, which is why it is staked at 0.5 points.

How to think about accas

  • Small stakes only. Long-odds accas go long runs without landing.
  • Each leg should be value on its own. A leg priced below its fair chance drags the whole acca down.
  • Never chase. A losing acca is the normal result, not a near miss.
