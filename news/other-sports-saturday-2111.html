Saturday night: Alcaraz v Shelton and Zverev v Medvedev in the Turin semi-finals, plus ten NHL and six NBA games

The ATP Finals semi-finals headline a busy Saturday away from football, with a full NHL card, an NBA slate that starts in the afternoon and a long college basketball night. Every main event with its chance on the sharp market.

Saturday away from football starts in Turin, where the ATP Finals reach the semi-finals: Carlos Alcaraz v Ben Shelton at 1.30pm UK and Alexander Zverev v Daniil Medvedev at 7.30pm. In North America there are ten NHL games and six NBA games, the first of them in the UK teatime slot, plus a long night of college basketball.

Every chance below comes from Pinnacle, the sharpest bookmaker in the market, with its margin removed, from BetMeisters' scan at 11am UK. They show what the market thinks, not what will happen. Times are UK, with US Eastern in brackets for the American games.

Tennis: ATP Finals semi-finals, Turin

Carlos Alcaraz v Ben Shelton (1.30pm): Alcaraz is 79.8% (fair price 1.25). Shelton is 20.2% (4.95).

Alexander Zverev v Daniil Medvedev (7.30pm): the closer semi. Zverev is 61.3% (1.63) and Medvedev 38.7% (2.58).

Elsewhere, Coco Gauff plays Dalma Galfi in the Billie Jean King Cup at 6.30pm and is priced at 98%.

NBA (six games)

  • Philadelphia 76ers v Milwaukee Bucks, 6pm (1pm ET): 76ers 84.1%. The early game, and a big favourite at home.
  • New York Knicks v Portland Trail Blazers, 12.30am (7.30pm ET): Knicks 65.4%.
  • Dallas Mavericks v LA Clippers, 1am (8pm ET): Mavericks 65.9%.
  • Minnesota Timberwolves v Memphis Grizzlies, 1am: Timberwolves 66.9%.
  • San Antonio Spurs v New Orleans Pelicans, 1am: Spurs 91.6%, the biggest favourite of the night.
  • Sacramento Kings v Boston Celtics, 3am (10pm ET): Celtics 87.3% on the road.

NHL (ten games)

  • Carolina Hurricanes v Edmonton Oilers, 6pm (1pm ET): Hurricanes 64.2%.
  • Calgary Flames v Chicago Blackhawks, 8.30pm: Flames 59.5%.
  • Nashville Predators v Pittsburgh Penguins, 11pm: Predators 55.6%.
  • Midnight (7pm ET) wave: Canadiens 59.0% v Flyers; Senators 61.8% v Kings; Red Wings v Islanders almost a coin flip (Islanders 51.4%); Devils 55.2% at the Blues; Golden Knights 55.0% at the Jets.
  • Late games, 3am: Wild 72.6% at the Canucks; Sharks v Kraken a near coin flip (Sharks 51.8%).

Hockey money lines include overtime and shoot-outs, which is why so many games sit between 45% and 60%.

College basketball: the closest games

  • Milwaukee v St. Thomas (MN), 8pm: St. Thomas 53.2%, the tightest game on the board.
  • Wichita State v New Mexico, 7pm: New Mexico 57.5% on the road.
  • George Mason v South Florida, 9pm: George Mason 57.5%.
  • Indiana State v Middle Tennessee, 8pm: Middle Tennessee 57.8%.
  • Missouri State v Drake, 8pm: Drake 60.5%.
