Slots took £4.8 billion online in a year: what the official figures say about Britain's online casinos

Online casino games made £5.7 billion from British players in 2025/26, more than double remote sports betting, and slots were 84% of it. Customer balances fell 14%. Here is what the Gambling Commission's figures show.

Online casinoUK
By the BetMeisters desk24 November 20264 min read

Online casino is now the biggest part of British gambling by far. The Gambling Commission's industry statistics for April 2025 to March 2026 show online casino games took £5.7 billion in gross gambling yield (what operators keep after paying out winnings) from British players, and £4.8 billion of that came from slots. Remote sports betting took £2.4 billion, less than half as much.

The key numbers

  • Online casino, betting and bingo together: £8.3 billion, up 6.9% on the year before.
  • Online casino games: £5.7 billion, of which slots £4.8 billion, about 84%.
  • Remote betting: £2.4 billion, led by football (£1.2 billion) and horse racing (£769 million). Remote bingo: £148 million.
  • New online accounts fell 3.0% to 32.4 million; there were 25.7 million active accounts at the end of the year.
  • Money held in customer accounts fell 13.9% to £886.6 million.

The Commission's Director of Policy, Ian Angus, said in a speech on 19 November that online casino growth was "basically driving the entire headline figure" for the sector: online casino yield rose by £736 million, more than the £728 million rise for all licensed gambling together.

The rules that now apply to slots

These figures cover the first full year of several new UK protections for online slot players:

  • Stake limits: £5 a spin for adults aged 25 and over, and £2 a spin for 18 to 24 year olds.
  • Bonus rules: since January 2026, wagering requirements on UK bonuses are capped at 10 times the bonus, and offers that mix casino and betting are banned. Our guide to the 10x cap explains what that means for you.
  • Game design: features such as turbo play and celebrating wins that are smaller than the stake are already banned on UK online slots.

What it means for players

Slots are where the industry makes most of its money because, over time, the house edge always wins. Every slot has a published return to player (RTP), often between about 94% and 97%, but many games come in several versions with different returns, and each casino chooses which one to run. Our RTP guide explains how to check.
