Reading tonight's numbers

Three things are worth knowing before a night like this.

  • Spreads are set to split opinion, not to predict the margin exactly. When the market makes Houston −7.5 a 50/50 proposition, it is saying that a Rockets win by eight or more is about as likely as anything else. A favourite at 72.5% to win can still be a coin flip against the spread.
  • Half-points matter. A spread of −1 or a total of 222 can push, with the stake returned, if the game lands exactly on the number; −1.5 or 222.5 cannot. In close NBA games a single free throw decides it.
  • Totals include overtime. An overtime period adds points to a basketball total, a small boost for overs in close games. Hockey totals and money lines include overtime and shootouts unless they say otherwise.

A month into the NBA season, the market still leans partly on pre-season ratings, and each result moves it, which is why lines can shift by a point or two through the day, especially when injury news lands.
