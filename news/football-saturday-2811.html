What stands out

  • The biggest favourites: Paris Saint-Germain (82% against Lorient), Bayern Munich (78% at Hamburg) and Inter (76% against Genoa). At those chances the fair prices are around 1.23, 1.29 and 1.32: little value on the result, so watch handicaps and goals instead.
  • The closest: Paderborn v Schalke (37% v 35%, with a 28% draw) and Wrexham v Portsmouth, where the draw is rated 29%.
  • Goals: PSG v Lorient (68%), Inter v Genoa (63%) and Mainz v Union Berlin (62%) are the likeliest to see three goals or more; Wrexham v Portsmouth (40%) and Le Mans v Lille (44%) the least.
  • Premier League: Leeds are 65% to beat Coventry, Manchester United 44% at home to Brentford, and Aston Villa 48% at Ipswich.
