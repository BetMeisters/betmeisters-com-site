Our view in one minute

Sky Bet is built around football and the app. Its free racing streams need no bet, its bet builder tools (Build A Bet, Ghost Leg, Acca Freeze) are among the most inventive in UK betting, and its apps are highly rated. Its weak spots on the checks done so far: no e-wallets such as PayPal, best odds guaranteed only for Sky Bet Club qualifiers, and no phone line listed. One important gap: Sky Bet is not in our price feed yet, so we have not scored its prices. That part of the score is missing, not neutral, so treat the number with care until we can.

Who Sky Bet suits

  • Football bettors who like bet builders: Build A Bet, Ghost Leg and Super Sub are built for Saturday afternoons.
  • Racing watchers: free UK and Irish racing streams with no bet required.
  • App users: both apps are rated 4.6 or above, as reported.

Who should look elsewhere: anyone who wants to pay with PayPal or another e-wallet, or who wants best odds guaranteed on every racing bet.

Odds and value: not yet measured

Our price comparisons so far cover bet365, William Hill, Paddy Power and Ladbrokes. Sky Bet is not in that feed yet, so we cannot say how its margins compare. We will add it when we can, and the score will move up or down with the result. Until then, compare its price with others before you bet.

Markets and features

Football is the centrepiece. Build A Bet is the bet builder. Ghost Leg removes one losing leg from an eligible Build A Bet of five or more selections (pre-match, combined odds 3/1 or bigger). Acca Freeze locks in a winning leg of a 5+ leg full-time-result acca during play, up to three times a week. Super Sub carries a player bet over if the player is substituted. Cash out is available on singles and multiples, including partial cash out. There is no separately named acca insurance.

Racing terms

The review we read says Sky Bet offers best odds guaranteed on UK and Irish racing from 9am, but only for Sky Bet Club qualifiers, not for every customer. That is why we score it below Paddy Power and Ladbrokes, which offer it to everyone from 8am. Another published review says extra places are offered on many races; we have not yet assessed each-way terms. UK and Irish racing streams are free once you are logged in, with no bet needed.

Payments and withdrawals

Sky Bet takes Visa, Mastercard and Maestro debit cards, Apple Pay, Pay by Bank and Revolut Pay, with a £5 minimum. The review we read says PayPal and Google Pay were recently withdrawn, so there are no e-wallets. Reported withdrawal times are instant for Pay by Bank and Revolut Pay after the first withdrawal, and 1 to 4 hours for eligible cards, otherwise 2 to 5 working days. Full ID checks are needed before your first withdrawal. We will time a real withdrawal ourselves.

The welcome offer

At the time of writing, new customers who deposit £10 and place a first bet of £10 at evens or bigger get five £10 free bets: three for football Build A Bets and two for football accas. They expire after 14 days, the stake is not returned with winnings, and Skrill or Neteller deposits do not qualify. Because they are tied to football bet builders and accas, they are worth well under £50 (our free bets guide shows the maths). Offers change often: check the current terms before you sign up.

Licence and safety

Sky Bet's sportsbook now runs under SBG Sports Limited, Gambling Commission account 67370, based in Malta, with a licence active since 6 October 2025 for betting on real and virtual events, pool betting and gambling software. www.skybet.com and m.skybet.com are listed on it. Sky Betting & Gaming has been part of the Flutter group since 2020.

The register shows no regulatory actions for SBG Sports Limited. The previous licence holder, Bonne Terre Limited, was fined £1.17 million in March 2022 after a Sky Vegas promotional email went to more than 41,000 self-excluded customers. That is outside the three-year window we score, but we mention it for completeness.

Safer gambling and support

Sky Bet offers deposit limits, a profit-and-loss tool, time-outs from 24 hours to 30 days and self-exclusion from six months to permanent, with GAMSTOP for UK customers. Customers under 25 get a default £500 monthly deposit limit, which is a sensible protection. Support is by live chat (one reviewer reached a human within two minutes), email and social media. The review we read lists no phone line or opening hours, so we score support a little lower until we check.
