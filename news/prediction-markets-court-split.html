Courts split on prediction markets: what the Kalshi rulings mean

Three federal appeals courts, two answers. The question of who regulates sports event contracts may be heading to the Supreme Court.

Sweepstakes and prediction marketsLicensing and regulation
By the BetMeisters desk16 November 20265 min read
Where it's legalChecked 16 Nov 2026
Prediction-market sports contracts are offered in many US states under federal (CFTC) oversight, but several states are fighting this in court and rulings differ by circuit. Availability can change quickly. Outside the US these platforms may be restricted or treated as gambling; check your local rules. 18+ (21+ in some places).

Prediction markets such as Kalshi let users buy contracts that pay out if an event happens, including the result of a sports match. Kalshi argues these are financial contracts regulated only by the Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC). Several states say they are sports betting and fall under state gambling law.

Three courts, two answers

  • Third Circuit (April 2026): in a 2-1 preliminary ruling, the court said New Jersey cannot stop Kalshi offering sports event contracts while the case continues, finding federal commodities law likely pre-empts state law. The dissent said the products are sports gambling that states can regulate.
  • Ninth Circuit (August 2026): held that Kalshi's contracts are subject to Nevada's gambling laws.
  • Sixth Circuit (25 September 2026): a unanimous panel ruled that Ohio and Tennessee can regulate the contracts under their gambling laws.

When federal appeals courts disagree like this, the US Supreme Court is more likely to take the case. Until then, whether a platform can offer sports contracts may depend on which circuit a state sits in.

Polymarket too

New York has also sued Polymarket over alleged illegal gambling; Polymarket has said it will fight for its users.

What it means for you

If you use these platforms, the rules where you live matter more than the platform's marketing. We will keep a state-by-state table on this page and date every check.
