Our view in one minute

Paddy Power is one of the biggest names in UK betting, with a strong racing offer (best odds guaranteed from 8am and daily extra-place races), full limit tools and 24/7 support. Two things hold its score back. On our own price data it was the dearest of the four big UK bookmakers we tracked, with an average margin above 10%. And its licence holder agreed a £2 million settlement with the Gambling Commission in December 2025 over customer-protection failings.

Who Paddy Power suits

  • Racing fans: best odds guaranteed on UK and Irish racing from 8am, plus extra-place races every day.
  • Bet builder fans: a Multi-Match Bet Builder lets you combine selections from different games.
  • App users: its iOS app is among the highest rated in UK betting.

Who should look elsewhere: anyone who bets mainly on price. On our scan its prices were further from the fair price than bet365 or Ladbrokes.

Odds and value: what our data shows

We compared UK bookmaker prices against the sharp Pinnacle market on 16 November 2026. Paddy Power's average margin was 10.15%, the highest of the four big bookmakers we tracked (bet365 8.48%, Ladbrokes 9.00%, William Hill 9.44%). Its prices averaged 12.1% below the fair price. It still had the best UK price on 27% of the selections we compared, about its fair share, so it is worth checking on individual bets. But as a default bookmaker it was the most expensive of the four. This is one day of data; we will update it as we collect more.

Markets and features

Coverage is led by football and horse racing. The Bet Builder works within one match, and the Multi-Match Bet Builder combines legs from different games. Acca boosts run regularly, with bigger boosts for more selections. The review we read lists live streaming but gives no detail, and does not mention acca insurance, so we have not scored either as a strength.

Racing terms

Paddy Power offers best odds guaranteed on UK and Irish horse racing from 8am on race day: if the starting price is bigger than the price you took, you are paid at the bigger price. It also runs daily extra-place races, where more places pay out each way than the standard terms. Each-way terms are set race by race, and we have not yet assessed them.

Payments and withdrawals

The method list covers what UK bettors use: debit cards, bank transfer, Apple Pay, PayPal, Skrill, Neteller and MuchBetter. One published review reports bank transfers often paid within an hour, e-wallets within a few hours and debit cards in two to five working days. We will time a real withdrawal ourselves before scoring speed.

The welcome offer

At the time of writing, new UK customers who bet £10 on a football Bet Builder at odds of 1/2 or bigger get five £10 football Bet Builder free bets. The qualifying bet must be placed within 30 days of opening the account, the free bets last 30 days, and deposits by PayPal, Skrill, Paysafe or Neteller do not qualify. Because the free bets can only be used on Bet Builders and the stake is not returned with winnings, they are worth well under £50 (our free bets guide shows the maths). Offers change often: check the current terms before you sign up.

Terms: the small print that decides payouts

Not yet read, so not yet scored. Paddy Power's own website is not reachable from our checking tools, so we have not yet read its full terms. They are left out of the score until we do, which means they count neither for nor against Paddy Power.

When we read them we will report, in plain English and good or bad: caps on what one bet or one day can pay; how wide the right is to void bets or keep winnings; and how winning customers are treated, including stake limits. Until then, read the terms yourself before you deposit, especially the maximum-winnings section.

Licence and safety

Paddy Power's online site runs under PPB Counterparty Services Limited, Gambling Commission account 39439, based in Malta and part of the Flutter group (Betfair runs on the same licence). The licence covers general betting on real and virtual events, active since 2014 and 2017, and www.paddypower.com is listed on it.

The register records one action: on 17 December 2025 four Paddy Power Betfair licensees, including this one, agreed a settlement with a total £2,000,000 payment in lieu of a financial penalty, plus costs and a public statement. The Commission found failings in how they checked and interacted with customers who might be at risk of harm (Social Responsibility Code 3.4.3). That counts against Paddy Power in our score. Separately, the Racing Post reported a £490,000 penalty in May 2023 over a promotion sent to self-excluded customers; that is outside the three-year window we score, but we mention it for completeness.

Safer gambling and support

The tools are complete: deposit and loss limits, a profit-and-loss view, reality checks, a budget calculator, take-a-break and self-exclusion, with GAMSTOP for UK customers. Support runs by 24/7 live chat, plus phone, email, social media and a help centre. We will test the live chat ourselves before scoring answer quality.

FAQs

Is Paddy Power licensed in the UK? Yes: Gambling Commission account 39439, PPB Counterparty Services Limited.

Does Paddy Power offer best odds guaranteed? Yes, on UK and Irish horse racing from 8am on race day, according to the published review we read.

Is Paddy Power good value? Not on our data: its average margin was 10.15% on our 16 November scan, the highest of the four big UK bookmakers we tracked. It still had the best price on about a quarter of selections, so compare before you bet.

Has Paddy Power been fined? Its licence holder agreed a £2 million settlement with the Gambling Commission in December 2025, shared with three other Paddy Power Betfair licensees.
