Sunday night in the US: 11 NFL games, seven NBA games and the 49ers v Vikings

Seven early NFL games, three in the late window and Sunday Night Football, plus seven NBA and five NHL games. Pinnacle's chances with the margin removed, in UK and US Eastern times.

Sunday is the busiest night of the week in American sport: 11 NFL games, seven NBA games and five NHL games. Every chance below is Pinnacle's price with the margin removed, taken through odds-api.io at about 4pm UK on Sunday. Times are UK first, with US Eastern Time (ET) in brackets. A spread or total quoted is the line where Pinnacle has the two sides closest to 50-50.

NFL early games (6pm UK, 1pm ET)

  • Buffalo Bills v Miami Dolphins: the biggest favourites of the day. Miami are only 7.5%, so Buffalo are about 92.5%, with a spread of Bills −16.5 and a total of 45.
  • Kansas City Chiefs v Arizona Cardinals: Kansas City about 92% (Arizona 8.1%), Chiefs −15, and the lowest early total at 40.
  • Chicago Bears v New Orleans Saints: Chicago 85% (fair price 1.18), Saints +11, total 47.5.
  • Dallas Cowboys v Tennessee Titans: Dallas 78%, Titans +7.5, and the highest early total at 54.5.
  • Detroit Lions v Tampa Bay Buccaneers: Detroit 65% (fair price 1.54), Lions −3.5, total 48.5.
  • Carolina Panthers v Baltimore Ravens: one of the closer games. Carolina 57%, Baltimore 43%, Panthers −2.5, total 44.
  • Philadelphia Eagles v Pittsburgh Steelers: Pittsburgh 65%, Philadelphia 35%, Eagles +3.5, and a low total of 37.5.

NFL late games and Sunday Night Football

  • Los Angeles Chargers v New York Jets (9.05pm UK, 4.05pm ET): Chargers 61%, −3, total 42.5.
  • Denver Broncos v Las Vegas Raiders (9.25pm UK, 4.25pm ET): Denver 84%, Raiders +11, total 41.5.
  • New York Giants v Jacksonville Jaguars (9.25pm UK, 4.25pm ET): Jacksonville 72%, Giants +5.5, total 45.
  • San Francisco 49ers v Minnesota Vikings (1.20am UK Monday, 8.20pm ET, Sunday Night Football): San Francisco 62%, Minnesota 38%, 49ers −3.5, total 45.5.

NBA: seven games from 8.30pm UK

  • Atlanta Hawks v Detroit Pistons (8.30pm UK, 3.30pm ET): close to a coin flip. Detroit 53%, Atlanta 47%, Hawks +1.5, total 224.
  • Miami Heat v Oklahoma City Thunder (11pm UK, 6pm ET): Miami 61%, Heat −4, total 231.5.
  • Utah Jazz v Dallas Mavericks (midnight UK, 7pm ET): exactly 50-50 on the money line, total 224.5.
  • Washington Wizards v Orlando Magic (midnight UK, 7pm ET): Orlando 65%, Wizards +5, total 220.
  • Chicago Bulls v Denver Nuggets (midnight UK, 7pm ET): Denver 78%, Bulls +10, and the highest total of the night at 235.5.
  • New York Knicks v Indiana Pacers (1am UK, 8pm ET): New York 76%, Knicks −8.5, total 214.
  • Golden State Warriors v Boston Celtics (1.30am UK, 8.30pm ET): Boston 75%, Warriors +8.5, and the lowest total at 210.

NHL: five games

  • New York Rangers v Carolina Hurricanes (5.30pm UK, 12.30pm ET): Carolina 60%.
  • Tampa Bay Lightning v Buffalo Sabres (10pm UK, 5pm ET): Tampa Bay 66%.
  • Dallas Stars v Pittsburgh Penguins (midnight UK, 7pm ET): Dallas 68%.
  • St. Louis Blues v Edmonton Oilers (midnight UK, 7pm ET): St. Louis 54%.
  • Vancouver Canucks v Chicago Blackhawks (1am UK, 8pm ET): almost level, Chicago 51%.

Money-line chances in hockey include overtime and shootouts. Most totals sit at 6.5 goals.
