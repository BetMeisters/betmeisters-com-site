Saturday 21 November is the first full club Saturday after the international break. The football runs from 12.30pm to 8pm UK time (7.30am to 3pm Eastern). Every chance below is from Pinnacle's prices with the margin removed, taken on Friday at about 3pm UK through odds-api.io. The fair price is one divided by the chance.

Our weekend Premier League preview on Thursday set out every game in the round. The market has barely moved since then: no Saturday Premier League chance has shifted by more than two points.

Updated 09:35 UK: the morning market

Pinnacle's prices this morning (margin removed) have hardly moved since Friday afternoon. The one Premier League game that has shifted is Chelsea v Leeds (3pm): Chelsea have eased from 50% to about 46%, with Leeds about 26% and the draw about 28%. Over 2.5 goals there is now about 50%.

Elsewhere: Tottenham are about 64% against Ipswich (from 62%), Arsenal about 62% at Newcastle (from 63%), Manchester City about 71% against Fulham and Coventry v Crystal Palace still close at 38% to 33%. In the Championship West Ham have eased a little to about 77% against Preston. Dortmund are about 62% at Gladbach, Real Betis 40% in the Seville derby and Real Madrid about 85% against Celta.

We have not added team news: none of it had been confirmed by a source we trust at the time of writing.

Newcastle v Arsenal (5.30pm UK, 12.30pm Eastern) is the game of the day. Arsenal are 63% to win at St James' Park (fair price 1.59), up a point since Thursday, with Newcastle at 16% and the draw at 22%. Manchester City are 70% at home to Fulham in the early game. The closest Premier League game is Coventry v Crystal Palace: 38% against 35%, with the draw at 27%.

Championship: the 12.30 starts and a West Ham home game

Three Championship games kick off at 12.30pm. Southampton are 72% at home to Derby, Norwich 54% against Cardiff, and Millwall v QPR is as close as it gets: 37% against 36%. At 3pm, West Ham are the biggest favourites in the division at 80% against Preston, with three or more goals 69% likely. Middlesbrough (70% v Bristol City) and Sheffield United (62% v Watford) are also clear favourites, while Lincoln v Wrexham is level at 35% and 36%, with over 2.5 goals only 45%, one of the lowest of the day.

Bundesliga: Gladbach v Dortmund (2.30pm UK)

Dortmund are 61% to win at Borussia-Park, with Gladbach at 18%. RB Leipzig are 65% at Union Berlin and Leverkusen 77% at home to Paderborn, with three or more goals 72% likely there. Eintracht Frankfurt v Hoffenheim is the open one (40% to 36%), and it is also 72% to have three or more goals. At 5.30pm, Stuttgart are 53% at Schalke.

LaLiga: the Seville derby (5.30pm UK)

Sevilla v Real Betis has the visitors as slight favourites: Betis 40%, Sevilla 32%, the draw 28%. Over 2.5 goals is 51%. In the late game Real Madrid are 84% at home to Celta Vigo (8pm UK, 3pm Eastern), the shortest-priced favourite of any game here. Earlier, Rayo Vallecano are 50% against Valencia (3.15pm) and Alavés v Deportivo La Coruña (1pm) is 44% to 28%.

Serie A, Ligue 1 and Scotland

Italy: Roma are 75% at Parma (5pm UK), Napoli 65% at home to Torino (7.45pm), and Como (74% v Cagliari) and Lazio (70% v Lecce) play at 2pm. France: PSG are 66% at Nice (4.15pm), with three or more goals 69% likely. At 7.45pm, Monaco are 68% v Auxerre and Lens 50% v Toulouse. Scotland: Celtic are 83% at home to St Mirren (3pm), Rangers 62% at Motherwell (8pm), and Kilmarnock v Aberdeen is level at 37% and 36%.

How to read these numbers

A chance is not a tip. A 70% favourite still fails to win about three times in ten. A price is worth more than the market thinks only when it is bigger than the fair price (for 40%, anything above 2.50). Our guide to reading odds explains how to turn a price into a chance.
