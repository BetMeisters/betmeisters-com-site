Arsenal v Man City prediction and odds: what the sharp market says, team news and likely scores

First v third at the Emirates on Sunday 29 November. The sharp market's chances with the margin removed, Raya and Zubimendi team news, the expected goals, the most likely scores and why no UK price beats the fair price yet. A preview, not a tip.

Arsenal against Manchester City is the biggest fixture of the Premier League weekend: first against third, the leaders six points clear, at the Emirates on Sunday 29 November (kick-off 16:30 UK). This is our full preview: the sharp betting market's view of the match with the bookmakers' margin removed, the team news, the form behind both sides, the likely scorelines, and what would make a price worth taking. This is a preview, not a tip. When we publish a tip, it appears on the tips page with its price, fair price and edge.

Arsenal v Man City prediction: the market's view

The sharpest betting market (the prices that professional bettors move) makes Arsenal clear favourites. With the bookmakers' margin removed, the chances are:

ResultChanceFair priceFractional (nearest)
Arsenal win47.6%2.1011/10
Draw26.1%3.8311/4

So Arsenal win about 48% of the time, and the draw and a City win are roughly equal at about one in four each. Over 2.5 goals is a 54% chance. Those are the market's numbers on Saturday afternoon; they will move with team news on Sunday.

The table and the form

Arsenal lead the Premier League on 28 points from 11 games (9 wins, 1 draw, 1 defeat), six points clear of Brighton and Manchester City, who both have 22. City's record is 7 wins, 1 draw and 3 defeats. Both sides arrive after a week of draws: Arsenal drew 2-2 with Newcastle on 21 November and 1-1 with Borussia Dortmund on Tuesday; City drew 1-1 with Fulham and 2-2 with Napoli in the same week.

Arsenal's lead is built on consistency rather than goals: their goal difference (+10) is smaller than Brighton's (+17), which suggests a side winning tight games. City's three league defeats already match what they lost in some whole title-winning seasons, and their recent run (one win in their last five matches in all competitions, per theScore) is the main reason the market rates Arsenal so highly at home.

Team news

Arsenal. Goalkeeper David Raya missed Friday's training with a shoulder problem and is a doubt. Martín Zubimendi is out for several weeks with a hamstring injury, a real loss in midfield. Bukayo Saka has returned to team training after a thigh problem, and William Saliba is back training after a back injury, with Mikel Arteta saying he will 'have minutes in the next few games'.

Manchester City. Antoine Semenyo is close to a return from a knee injury but manager Enzo Maresca said he hoped to have him back 'next week', so Sunday looks too soon.

Team news matters more than usual here. A change of goalkeeper and Zubimendi's absence are not fully known quantities, and the market will react when the line-ups appear an hour before kick-off. If Raya is ruled out, expect Arsenal's price to drift slightly.

How many goals?

Working backwards from the match and goals prices, the market expects Arsenal to score about 1.62 goals and City about 1.19, around 2.8 goals in total. That is a fairly open game for a top-of-the-table meeting. Both teams score about 56% of the time on these numbers.

Clean sheets and win-to-nil come from the goals model, which slightly underrates low-scoring draws, so treat those as a little generous to the goals side. If a bookmaker's price on any of these is above the fair price shown, that is the first sign of value; then check it with team news and a second bookmaker.

How to use this preview

  1. Wait for the line-ups about an hour before kick-off, especially Arsenal's goalkeeper.
  2. Compare any price with the fair price above, not with how likely the result feels.
  3. Shop around: the same bet can differ by several percent between UK bookmakers; our margin index shows who usually prices closest to fair.
  4. Keep the stake sensible: even a 48% favourite loses more often than it wins. Our value betting guide explains why price matters more than the result.

What would make a bet worth taking

  • Arsenal are value only above about 2.10 (11/10); the best UK price is below that.
  • The draw needs about 3.83 (11/4) or better.
  • City need about 3.81 (14/5) or better.
  • Team news can change all of these. If a price moves above fair after the line-ups, it is worth a second look; if it moves below, the market has probably learned something.

Head to head

The last meeting listed was on 19 April 2026, when City won 2-1. Big-match history is a poor guide on its own, though: both squads and City's manager have changed since many of the famous meetings, and the market's view already includes everything the form says.
