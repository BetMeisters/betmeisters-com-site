Our view in one minute

Ladbrokes is one of the best-known names in British betting and, on the checks done so far, one of the cleanest: its Gambling Commission account shows no regulatory actions, it offers best odds guaranteed on UK and Irish racing from 8am, and its acca and bet builder tools are as complete as anyone's. On price it sits in the middle of the big four we track. On the evidence so far its desk review score is 8.1 out of 10, with 41% of our checks complete.

Who Ladbrokes suits

  • Racing fans: BOG from 8am and free UK racing streams with an active account.
  • Acca and bet builder bettors: Bet Builder+ lets you combine legs from different matches and sports, with a daily acca boost and acca insurance.
  • Anyone who wants a big, regulated UK brand with a clean record.

Who should look elsewhere: bettors who chase the best price on every football and tennis market. On our data bet365 was cheaper on average.

Odds and value: what our data shows

We compared UK bookmaker prices against the sharp Pinnacle market on 16 and 17 November 2026. Ladbrokes' average margin was 9.0% on 16 November (90 main-market selections) and 8.6% on 17 November (156), against bet365's 8.5% and 7.7%. Its prices averaged about 11% below the fair price over the two scans: better than William Hill and Paddy Power, behind bet365.

The good news is breadth: Ladbrokes had the best UK price on 43.2% of the selections we compared on 17 November, second only to bet365 and well above a 25% fair share. Two days is a small sample, and we will keep scanning daily before this score is final.

Markets and features

Coverage is led by football and horse racing, with cricket, tennis and a long list of smaller sports. The bet builder range is a strength: Bet Builder for one match and Bet Builder+ for legs across different matches and sports, plus a daily acca boost and acca insurance (stake back up to £10 as a free bet on a 5+ fold football acca). UK horse and greyhound racing streams free with any active account; Irish and international races need a £1 bet first. We have not yet counted the number of sports ourselves.

Racing terms

Ladbrokes offers best odds guaranteed on UK and Irish horse racing from 8am on race day: if the starting price is bigger than the price you took, you are paid at the bigger price. That is two hours earlier than William Hill's 10am. Each-way terms and extra places are set race by race, and we have not yet assessed them.

Payments and withdrawals

The method list covers what UK bettors use: Visa, Mastercard and Maestro debit cards, PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay, Pay by Bank and paysafecard, with no withdrawal fees. One published review reports card withdrawals within four hours through Fast Funds (one to three banking days for banks that do not take part), e-wallets in about eight hours and bank transfers in two to four banking days. We do not score speed until we have made our own test withdrawal.

The welcome offer

New UK customers who bet £5 at odds of 1/2 or bigger within 14 days get six £5 free bets: four for any sports market and two for football Bet Builder. The free bets expire after seven days and the stake is not returned with winnings, so they are worth well under £30 (our free bets guide shows the maths). PayPal, Skrill, Neteller, paysafecard, pre-paid cards and some debit cards do not qualify. Average value with some strings: 5 out of 10.

One note for our follow-up: the same published review lists a casino bonus with 20x wagering. UK rules have capped wagering at 10x since January 2026, so we will check the current terms on the site itself before we score anything on the casino side.

Terms: the small print that decides payouts

Not yet read, so not yet scored. Ladbrokes's own website is not reachable from our checking tools, so we have not yet read its full terms. They are left out of the score until we do, which means they count neither for nor against Ladbrokes.

When we read them we will report, in plain English and good or bad: caps on what one bet or one day can pay; how wide the right is to void bets or keep winnings; and how winning customers are treated, including stake limits. Until then, read the terms yourself before you deposit, especially the maximum-winnings section.

Licence and safety

Ladbrokes' online site runs under LC International Limited, Gambling Commission account 54743, based in Gibraltar and part of the Entain group (its sister brands include Coral). The licence covers betting, casino, bingo, pool betting and betting intermediary services, all active since 1 July 2019, and ladbrokes.com is listed on it. The register shows no regulatory actions for this account. How customer money is protected is not yet confirmed: we will read it in the terms.

Safer gambling and support

The tools are complete: deposit limits, deposit curfews, reality checks, loss limits, time-outs from 24 hours to six weeks, and self-exclusion from six months to five years, with GAMSTOP for UK customers. Support runs 24/7 by live chat, plus phone, email and social media; one reviewer reached a human agent in under a minute, but also found the chat hard to find on the site.

FAQs

Is Ladbrokes licensed in the UK? Yes: Gambling Commission account 54743, LC International Limited.

Does Ladbrokes offer best odds guaranteed? Yes, on UK and Irish horse racing from 8am on race day, according to the published review we read.

Is Ladbrokes good value? Middle of the pack: its average margin was 8.6% on our 17 November data, higher than bet365 but lower than William Hill and Paddy Power.
