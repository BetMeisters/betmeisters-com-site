Meister previews · Tuesday 24 November

Tuesday's racing: a valuable Wolverhampton sprint, two Class 3 maidens and big fields at Wincanton across five meetings

Lingfield, Southwell and Wincanton in the afternoon, Tramore in Ireland, then Wolverhampton under the lights. Every meeting, its going and the races to watch.

Tuesday's racing runs from 11.38am to 8.30pm UK time (6.38am to 3.30pm Eastern) across five meetings and 39 races: three in Britain in the afternoon, one in Ireland, and an evening all-weather card at Wolverhampton. Runners, going and prize money are from the racecards published on Monday afternoon. Most bookmakers had not priced the races yet, so this card has no chances; they will come with any tips.

The meetings

  • Lingfield (all-weather, standard): 9 races from 11.38am to 3.38pm, mostly Class 6 handicaps and nurseries, with a Class 3 maiden at 1.38pm the best prize (£6,480).
  • Southwell (jumps, good to soft): 8 races from 11.56am to 3.26pm, closing with a Class 3 handicap hurdle.
  • Wincanton (jumps, good to firm): 6 races from 12.53pm to 3.23pm, with big fields: 19, 21 and 20 runners in three of the hurdles.
  • Tramore (Ireland, jumps, soft): 7 races from 12.45pm to 3.50pm; the feature is a mares' maiden hurdle at 1.15pm worth €9,000.
  • Wolverhampton (all-weather, Tapeta, standard): 9 races from 4.30pm to 8.30pm, including the richest race of the day in Britain.

Wolverhampton 7pm · Class 3 handicap, 6f, 10 runners (£7,956)

The best prize in Britain on Tuesday is a sprint handicap. Julie Camacho's Desert Falcon (rated 92) is top of the ratings, just ahead of Ralph Beckett's Bolo Neighs (90). William Haggas runs Another Abbot (89, ran 53 days ago) and Simon Crisford Dandana (89). Clive Cox's Harry Three (87) is back after 822 days off the track.

Wolverhampton 5.30pm and 6pm · Class 3 maidens, 1m1f

Two divisions of a £7,020 maiden. The first has Charlie Appleby's Zajal (Billy Loughnane), second on his last run 32 days ago, against James Fanshawe's newcomer The Great Karoo and Ed Walker's Radio Bar. The second division has eight runners.

Lingfield 1.38pm · Class 3 maiden, 1m4f, 9 runners

William Haggas's Rowlf and Hughie Morrison's Secret Squirrel (third last time, 10 days ago) meet Charlie Johnston's Champion Lawman, who returns after 182 days.

Southwell 3.26pm · Class 3 handicap hurdle, 2m, 8 runners

Manuelito (Philip Hobbs and Johnson White, rated 128) tops the weights, from Jennie Candlish's Lawrenny (123, back after 199 days). Klitschko (117) has been second twice in his last three runs.

Tramore 1.15pm and Wincanton

At Tramore, Gordon Elliott's Marie D'angos (Jack Kennedy) runs in the €9,000 mares' maiden hurdle on soft ground. At Wincanton, the ground is good to firm and the fields are big: Paul Nicholls runs Babyken in the 19-runner novices' hurdle at 1.23pm, and the 1.53pm and 2.53pm handicaps have 21 and 20 runners.
