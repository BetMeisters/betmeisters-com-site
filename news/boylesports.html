Our view in one minute

BoyleSports is Ireland's biggest independent bookmaker and a strong all-rounder for UK and Irish bettors. On the checks done so far its record is clean: no regulatory actions on its Gambling Commission account, best odds guaranteed from 8am on UK and Irish racing, a wide range of payment methods and phone support around the clock, which few online bookmakers still offer. Its apps are rated lower than most rivals'. One important gap: BoyleSports is not in our price feed yet, so we have not scored its prices. That part of the score is missing, not neutral.

Who BoyleSports suits

  • Racing fans: best odds guaranteed from 8am, extra places every day and money back if your horse is second to the favourite.
  • Acca and Lucky 15 bettors: Acca Insure, acca boosts and Double the Odds when only one selection in a Lucky 15, 31 or 63 wins.
  • Bettors who like to phone: a freephone line open around the clock.

Who should look elsewhere: anyone who bets mostly in the app; both apps score well below the market leaders.

Odds and value: not yet measured

Our weekly margin table covers bet365, William Hill, Paddy Power and Ladbrokes. BoyleSports is not in that feed yet, so we cannot say how its margins compare. We will add it when we can, and the score will move up or down with the result. Until then, compare its price with others before you bet.

Markets and features

Football and horse racing lead, with greyhounds and a long list of smaller sports. The bet builder comes with a Bet Builder Boost (25% or 50% on winning bets at 3/1 or bigger, up to £1,000). Acca Insure refunds the stake as a free bet (up to £20) if one leg of a five-fold or bigger loses. Early Payout settles a bet as a winner if your team goes two goals ahead at any point. Sub Swap moves a player bet to the substitute in the main European leagues. Cash out is not available on Bet Builder, Squad Builder or Sub Swap bets.

Racing terms

BoyleSports offers best odds guaranteed on UK and Irish horse racing from 8am on race day, for win and each-way bets up to £50,000 a day. It adds extra-place races daily, gives money back as a free bet (up to £10) if your horse finishes second to the starting-price favourite, and pays double the odds on a Lucky 15, 31 or 63 when every selection runs but only one wins. These are among the most generous racing terms we have reviewed so far.

Payments and withdrawals

The method list is wide: Visa and Mastercard debit, PayPal, Skrill, Neteller, Paysafecard, Apple Pay, Google Pay and bank transfer, with deposits from £5. Withdrawals start at £10. A published review reports withdrawals processed within six hours, then two to five working days to the bank, or within four hours with Fast Funds on eligible Visa and Mastercard cards. We will time a real withdrawal ourselves before scoring speed.

The welcome offer

At the time of writing, new UK mobile customers (not Northern Ireland) who deposit £10 and place a £10 bet at evens or bigger get a £30 free bet, credited within minutes of the qualifying bet settling. It expires after seven days, and deposits by Skrill, Neteller, PayPal, Paysafecard or prepaid card do not qualify. As with all free bets, the stake is not returned, so it is worth less than £30 (our free bets guide shows the maths). Offers change often: check the current terms before you sign up.

Terms: the small print that decides payouts

We have read BoyleSports' general terms (effective 22 June 2026) and its betting rules (25 November 2026). They are long and legal in style, with a short plain-English summary at the top. What matters most for a bettor:

  • Maximum payouts: high, but they depend on the sport. Main markets in the Premier League and other top leagues pay up to £1,000,000, other markets there £50,000, and lower-level football only £10,000 (£5,000 on other markets). UK Class 5-6 races pay up to £100,000 and US sports £100,000. Bets on the same selections, spread across several bets, days or accounts, are capped at one payout.
  • Restricting winners: BoyleSports can refuse or limit any bet for any reason (11.4.3), and can restrict offers and price concessions customer by customer without notice. The terms name customers who arrive via Oddschecker as an example (8.5.4).
  • Errors: if odds or winnings are shown wrongly, it can decline to pay out, even if nobody noticed at the time. This is standard UK wording, but it is broad.
  • Software and VPNs: using bots or automated systems for an advantage, or a VPN to hide your location, is prohibited conduct (9.1.4, 9.1.8).
  • Your money: customer funds are held separately and rated Medium protection by the Gambling Commission scheme (10.1.3). An account becomes dormant after 24 months without activity; you get at least 30 days' notice and the balance is held for you.
  • Disputes: if a complaint is not settled within eight weeks, you can take it to IBAS for free, binding adjudication (28.3).

Overall: generous payout limits on major events, but wide rights to limit winning customers, which value bettors should know about.

Licence and safety

BoyleSports' online business runs under Boylesports Enterprise, Gambling Commission account 39469, based in Dundalk, Ireland. Its remote licence covers betting on real and virtual events, pool betting, casino and bingo, all active since 1 November 2014, and boylesports.com is listed on it with its casino, games and bingo sites.

The register shows no regulatory actions for this licence holder. Sign-up uses an automated age and identity check with a selfie, and a published review says documents are usually verified within six hours.

Safer gambling and support

The tools are complete: deposit limits, reality checks, time-outs from 24 hours to six weeks, self-exclusion from six months to five years, a budget calculator and a self-assessment test, with GAMSTOP for UK customers. Support is the best we have seen so far: 24/7 live chat, a freephone line around the clock and email (usually answered within 12 hours, as reported).
