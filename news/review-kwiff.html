Our view in one minute

kwiff is a smaller UK bookmaker with one big idea: Supercharged bets. Any bet on any sport has a random chance of being boosted to bigger odds after you place it, and kwiff says it has paid more than £125 million on Supercharged winners since 2016. Its licence record is clean, its iOS app is highly rated and its safer-gambling tools go further than most, with curfew hours and product opt-outs. The trade-offs: no best odds guaranteed on racing, fewer sports than the biggest bookmakers, and no phone line. kwiff is not in our price feed yet, so we have not scored its prices.

Who kwiff suits

  • Football and acca bettors who like a surprise: any bet can be Supercharged, including each leg of a Lucky 15 or Yankee.
  • App users on iPhone: the iOS app is rated 4.5 from nearly 10,000 ratings, as reported.
  • Bettors who want firm limits: curfew hours and product opt-outs as well as deposit and loss limits.

Who should look elsewhere: racing bettors who rely on best odds guaranteed, and anyone who wants deep in-play markets across many sports.

Odds and value: not yet measured

Supercharged boosts are random, so they cannot be planned for and they do not change the price you are quoted when you bet. What matters for value is kwiff's normal price, and kwiff is not in our price feed yet, so we cannot say how its margins compare with the bookmakers in our weekly margin table. We will add it when we can. Until then, compare its price with others before you bet, and treat a Supercharge as a bonus rather than a reason to bet.

Markets and features

Beyond Supercharged bets, kwiff offers a Bet Builder and Bet Builder+, which combines selections from different matches; stake-back cover (up to £10 on Bet Builders and £20 on accas) when one leg loses; Flex Bet, which pays a reduced amount on selected accas and Bet Builders even if one pick loses; and cash out across most sports, which can itself be Supercharged. The review we read says it covers fewer sports and has thinner in-play markets than the largest rivals.

Racing terms

This is kwiff's weak spot. It does not offer best odds guaranteed on horse or greyhound racing, according to the review we read. There is a free bet of up to £10 if your horse finishes second to the favourite at selected meetings, and First Past The Post settlement on UK and Irish racing (with exclusions including the Grand National). Racing bettors will usually do better elsewhere.

Payments and withdrawals

kwiff takes Visa and Mastercard debit, PayPal, Revolut, Apple Pay and bank transfer, with £10 minimums. A published review reports withdrawal requests reviewed within 24 hours, with Apple Pay and Open Banking payouts usually arriving within minutes and some debit cards taking up to three business days. We will time a real withdrawal ourselves before scoring speed.

The welcome offer

At the time of writing, new UK customers who deposit £10 and place a £10 bet at evens or bigger get four £10 free bets: one single, two accas and one Bet Builder (accas and Bet Builders need three or more selections). They are credited within 48 hours, expire after seven days and need odds of evens or bigger. Winnings carry no wagering, but the stake is not returned, so they are worth well under £40 (our free bets guide shows the maths). Offers change often: check the current terms before you sign up.

Licence and safety

kwiff runs under Eaton Gate Gaming Limited, Gambling Commission account 44448, based in London. The licence covers betting on real events (since 2016) and virtual events, casino and gambling software, and lists kwiff.com, kwiff.co.uk, kwiffcasino.com and the kwiff apps. The register shows no regulatory actions for this licence holder.

Safer gambling and support

kwiff's tools go further than most: daily, weekly or monthly deposit and loss limits, curfew hours and product opt-outs, time-outs from 24 hours to six weeks and self-exclusion from six months to five years, with GAMSTOP for UK customers. Raising a limit takes 24 hours; lowering one is immediate. Support is by live chat (one reviewer reached an agent in one to three minutes), email and a help centre. No phone line or opening hours are listed.
