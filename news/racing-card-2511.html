Wednesday's racing: Constitution Hill runs in a Listed race on the Flat at Kempton, plus a £36,000 series final

Wetherby and Hereford over jumps, Newcastle and Dundalk on the all-weather, then an evening at Kempton with the two best prizes of the day. Every meeting, its going and the races to watch.

Wednesday's racing runs from 12.16pm to 8.30pm UK time (7.16am to 3.30pm Eastern) across five meetings and 40 races: two jumps meetings in the afternoon, all-weather racing at Newcastle and Dundalk, and an evening card at Kempton. Runners, going and prize money are from the racecards published on Tuesday afternoon. Prices are not settled yet, so this card has no chances; they will come with any tips.

The meetings

  • Wetherby (jumps, good): 7 races from 12.16pm to 3.16pm, mostly Class 4 hurdles and chases.
  • Hereford (jumps, good): 7 races from 12.31pm to 3.37pm, Class 4 and 5.
  • Newcastle (all-weather, standard): 9 races from 2.25pm to 6.30pm, with a Class 3 handicap at 3.55pm the best prize (£7,956).
  • Dundalk (Ireland, all-weather, standard): 8 races from 2.01pm to 5.40pm, including a €13,200 maiden at 3.01pm.
  • Kempton (all-weather, standard to slow): 9 races from 4.20pm to 8.30pm, with a Listed race at 7pm and a series final at 7.30pm.

Kempton 7pm · the Floodlit Stakes, Listed, 1m4f, 8 runners (£34,026)

The most interesting name on Wednesday's cards is Constitution Hill. Nicky Henderson's 2023 Champion Hurdle winner runs on the Flat in this Listed race, ridden by Oisin Murphy, 31 days after his last run. He is rated 106 on the Flat. Charlie Appleby's El Cordobes (Billy Loughnane) is top of the ratings on 114, with Ralph Beckett's Amiloc (Hollie Doyle) on 113. John and Thady Gosden run Lion's Pride (107) and Andrew Balding Royal Playwright (106, second on his last two runs).

Kempton 7.30pm · London Middle Distance Series final, Class 2 handicap, 1m2f, 14 runners (£36,078)

The richest race of the day is for horses who qualified through the series. Alan King's Paradias (Sean Levey) tops the weights on 105 but has not run for 95 days. Solarize (Charlie Johnston) and Haku (Billy Loughnane for Mark Loughnane) are next on 94, and Andrew Balding has two runners, Classical Allusion (Oisin Murphy) and Cape Fear.

Newcastle 3.55pm · Class 3 handicap, 1m, 13 runners (£7,956)

George Boughey's McMurray (rated 93) has won four of his last six starts. James Horton's Saytarr (92) returns after 123 days, and the Gosdens' Lighting Thunder (Cieren Fallon) and Richard Hannon's Lost Signal are also in the field.

Dundalk 3.01pm and Wetherby 1.16pm

At Dundalk, Joseph O'Brien has six entries in the €13,200 maiden, among them Sharpe Ratio, second on debut 12 days ago. At Wetherby, Jeteye (Sam England) runs in the 3m handicap chase five days after his last run, having won four of his last five starts.
