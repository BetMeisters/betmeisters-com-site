Iceland and Al Lahbab: tonight's Original Combo Meister double, by the numbers

A football and racing double at 2.88. The picks are fully manual; here are the stats behind each leg and what the double needs.

The Original Combo Meister is the manual side of our combos: the picks are made by hand, with no model involved, and we add the numbers afterwards. Tonight's double joins a Nations League handicap with a novice race at Wolverhampton.

Leg 1: Iceland -1 (three-way handicap) v Luxembourg, 17:00 UK

Iceland must win by two goals or more. A one-goal win counts as a draw on the three-way handicap, so it loses. The price is 1.666; the sharp market makes a two-goal Iceland win about 57.3%, a fair price of 1.75. That is a small negative edge of about 4.6%.

Leg 2: Al Lahbab (win), Wolverhampton 18:00 UK

Al Lahbab, trained by Charlie Appleby, is favourite for the 6pm novice. The price is 1.73; our fair line from the bookmaker market makes him about 54.8%, a fair price of 1.82. Again slightly short, by about 5%.

The double

Together the double pays 2.88, and the combined chance is about 31%, roughly 1 in 3. With both legs a little under fair, the double carries a negative edge of about 9.5%. That is the honest picture, and we publish it next to every Original Combo Meister pick so the record can be judged fairly over time.

Combos sit between singles and accas: two or three legs, a realistic chance of landing, and a record kept separately on BetMeisters.com, winners and losers.
