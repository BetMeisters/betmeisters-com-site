A spread or total is the line where the two sides are closest to 50-50. There are no NBA or NHL games on Thanksgiving Day.

NFL

Detroit Lions v Chicago Bears, 6pm (1pm ET) at Ford Field, was already under way as this was published.

Dallas Cowboys v Philadelphia Eagles, 9.30pm (4.30pm ET) at AT&T Stadium: Dallas are 79.3% to win, about 8.5-point favourites, with a total of 49.5 points.

Buffalo Bills v Kansas City Chiefs, 1.20am Friday (8.20pm ET) at New Highmark Stadium: Buffalo 60.6%, 3-point favourites, total 46. The spread sits right on 3: Kansas City +3 is 50.5%.

Overtime counts for the winner, spread and total markets.

College basketball: Feast Week

Most of these are neutral-site tournament games, so the first-named team is not necessarily at home.

  • Arizona State v South Carolina, 7pm (2pm ET): Arizona State 64.2%, about 4.5-point favourites, total 140.
  • Texas v Georgetown, 7.30pm (2.30pm ET): Texas 77.6%, about 8.5-point favourites, total 155.
  • Miami v Michigan, 8.30pm (3.30pm ET): close, Miami 51.5%, total 159.5.
  • Iowa State v Tennessee, 8.30pm (3.30pm ET): Iowa State 65.3%, about 4.5-point favourites, total 140.5.
  • Michigan State v Arkansas, 9.30pm (4.30pm ET): Michigan State 67.4%, about 5.5-point favourites.
  • UCLA v Saint Mary's, 10pm (5pm ET): UCLA 74.1%, about 7-point favourites, total 153.5.
  • Creighton v TCU, 11pm (6pm ET): close, Creighton 53.4%, total 153.5.
  • LSU v SMU, midnight (7pm ET): LSU 73.8%, about 7.5-point favourites.
  • St. John's v Louisville, 2am (9pm ET): a coin flip, St. John's 52.6%, total 160.5.
  • Alabama v Kansas State, 2am (9pm ET): Alabama 90.7%, about 13.5-point favourites, and the highest total on the card at 170.5.
  • Gonzaga v Baylor, 4.30am (11.30pm ET): Gonzaga 60.8%, about 3.5-point favourites, total 154.5.
  • Texas Tech v Oregon, 4.30am (11.30pm ET): Texas Tech 82.9%, about 10.5-point favourites.
