Our view in one minute

BitStarz is one of the longest-running crypto-friendly casinos. On paper it is transparent: the operating company, its company number and its Curaçao licence number are all in the first lines of its terms, and the Curaçao register lists that licence as "Indefinite", the cleanest status there is. Withdrawals are promised within an hour with no fees from BitStarz. Against that, the terms give the casino wide rights to withhold winnings, charge a monthly fee on dormant accounts, and name no independent body you can take a complaint to.

Licence and where it applies

Licence: Curaçao Gaming Authority, licence OGL/2024/165/0185, held by Gareton B.V. (company 160656, Willemstad, Curaçao). Register status on 20 November 2026: Indefinite, issued 24 December 2025.

Where you cannot play: the terms bar real-money play from a long list of countries, including the United Kingdom (and the Isle of Man, Jersey and Gibraltar), the United States, France, Spain, the Netherlands, Belgium, Israel, Portugal, Poland, Romania, Brazil and Russia, among many others (3.6). Some game providers block further countries on their own games (4.3).

Payments and withdrawals

  • Speed: BitStarz "shall try" to pay withdrawals within 1 hour, with a stated 10-minute average for most, longer when a big win is checked with the game provider (8.7.1, 8.7.8).
  • Fees: no withdrawal fees from BitStarz or its payment processor (8.7.10).
  • Play-through: every deposit must be wagered at least once before you can withdraw it, an anti-money-laundering rule (8.7.5).
  • Same method: withdrawals go back to the method you deposited with; USD depositors withdraw only to e-wallets, and bank transfers are paid in euros (8.7.7, 8.7.9, 8.7.13).
  • Identity checks: expect them before your first withdrawal; if BitStarz cannot reach you within two weeks of a withdrawal request, the account is locked (8.7.11).

Terms: the good and the bad

  • Dormant accounts: after six months without logging in, BitStarz can charge at least EUR 5 a month until the balance hits zero or you log back in (13.1, 13.2). Withdraw what you do not use.
  • Bonus play: if you hold back bonus features or game rounds until after your wagering is complete, BitStarz can withhold withdrawals and confiscate all winnings.
  • Crypto fees: if your crypto deposit is flagged as having too low a network fee, BitStarz reserves the right to confiscate winnings (9.6.6). Send deposits with a normal fee.
  • Wrong network: crypto sent to the wrong network address is only chased if it is over $5,000, and then a 0.1 BTC fee applies.
  • Disputes: complaints go to support and are escalated internally; after that you "may escalate" to the authority or a dispute service, but none is named (14.8). The casino's server records are final (14.4).

Support

Live chat and email around the clock (12.2). We have not yet tested response times ourselves, so they are not scored.

FAQs

Can I play at BitStarz from the UK? No. The UK is on its restricted list, and its Curaçao licence does not cover UK players.

Is its licence valid? The Curaçao register of 20 November 2026 lists Gareton B.V.'s licence as "Indefinite".

Are there withdrawal fees? Not from BitStarz, but a dormant account can be charged at least EUR 5 a month.
