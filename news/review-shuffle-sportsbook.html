Our view in one minute

Shuffle is a fast-growing crypto casino and sportsbook run by Natural Nine B.V. in Curaçao, and on the basics it is one of the cleaner offshore operators we have reviewed. Its terms name the company, its company number and its licence in the first lines, the Curaçao register lists that licence as "Indefinite", the strongest status there is, and the terms say player money is held separately from the company's own funds. It also promises not to use a responsible-gambling measure as a reason to hold a legitimate withdrawal.

The weaknesses are the usual offshore ones, sharpened in places: every deposit must be wagered once before withdrawal, Shuffle can void bets from arbitrage or matched bettors, its total liability to you is capped at as little as USD 100, and there is no independent dispute service, only a referral to the regulator. It is not available in the UK.

Licence and where it applies

Licence: Curaçao Gaming Authority, licence OGL/2024/1337/0628, held by Natural Nine B.V. (company 160998), Korporaalweg 10, Willemstad, Curaçao. Register of 20 November 2026: issued 24 December 2025, status Indefinite.

Where you cannot play: Australia, the British Virgin Islands, the Cayman Islands, Curaçao, Cyprus, France, Germany, the Netherlands, Portugal, Singapore, Spain, Sweden, the United Kingdom, the United States and anywhere else it would need a licence (8.3). Using a VPN to hide your location is a breach of the terms (8.4), and Shuffle checks device time zones and IP masking.

Payments and withdrawals

  • Play-through: you must wager 100% of each deposit before withdrawing it, except a crypto withdrawal back to the same wallet and network it came from (10.2).
  • Identity: a government ID check before your first withdrawal, and source-of-funds evidence on request (5.2, 5.4).
  • Fees: any Shuffle fee is shown before you confirm; network fees apply to crypto withdrawals (9.9, 10.7, 10.16).
  • Dormant accounts: no monthly fee; after 24 months of inactivity Shuffle emails you, and can close the account three months later (11.6).
  • Crypto care: a deposit on the wrong network or to an expired address may be lost for good (9.17).

The sportsbook

Shuffle's sports rules have several points every bettor should know before the first bet:

  • Win limit: a maximum of USD 500,000 credited from sports bets, and the same as a maximum per customer per day, unless Shuffle agrees otherwise (rules, Part B).
  • Overtime: match markets do not include overtime unless the market says so. Check before betting on NBA, NFL or NHL lines.
  • Tennis retirements: every market not already decided is void, the most bettor-friendly common rule (Part C, tennis).
  • Same Game Multis: if any leg is void, the whole bet is void, not just that leg.
  • Delays and integrity: events must finish within 48 hours or bets are void; payouts can be held during integrity checks, and settlements can be withheld where match-fixing is suspected.
  • Cash out: available on some markets, partial from 5% to 95%; not guaranteed, with a time delay, and it can cost bonus eligibility.

The biggest issue for value bettors is in the general terms: Shuffle may void bets placed through arbitrage or matched betting, or after "a significant change in odds" (12.4). That is common offshore, but it means a winning, price-sensitive bettor has fewer protections here than at a UK bookmaker.

Terms: the good and the bad

  • Good: player funds held separately from operating funds (11.1).
  • Good: responsible-gambling tools will not be used to stop a legitimate withdrawal, and funds stay withdrawable in a cooling-off period (10.10).
  • Good: no dormant-account fee.
  • Bad: total liability to you capped at the lowest of your balance, your deposits in the last 12 months or USD 100, except for gross negligence or fraud (16.3).
  • Bad: bets can be voided for arbitrage, matched betting or a significant odds change (12.4).
  • Bad: anyone sharing a household, device or IP address with another account holder must report it, and unreported linked accounts can be closed (4.3).
  • Bad: no independent dispute service; unresolved complaints go to the Curaçao Gaming Authority (18.2).

FAQs

Can I use Shuffle from the UK? No. The UK is a prohibited jurisdiction and the Curaçao licence does not cover UK players.

Is Shuffle's licence valid? The Curaçao register of 20 November 2026 lists Natural Nine B.V.'s licence as "Indefinite".

Is my money held separately? The terms say player deposits and winnings are held in accounts and wallets separate from Shuffle's operating funds (11.1).

What is the maximum I can win on sports? USD 500,000, and the same per day, unless Shuffle agrees otherwise.
