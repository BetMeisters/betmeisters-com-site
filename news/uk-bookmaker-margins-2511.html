How much do UK bookmakers keep? This week's margin table from 304 events

We took every match-result and money-line market four big UK bookmakers priced over the next 40 hours and worked out how much margin each builds in. Lower is better for you. Bet365 had the lowest average this week.

Sports betting
By the BetMeisters desk25 November 20263 min read

Every bookmaker builds a margin into its prices: add up the implied chances of every outcome in a market and you get more than 100%. The extra is what the bookmaker expects to keep. The lower the margin, the more of your stake comes back to you over time.

This week's table

Average margin on match-result (football, three outcomes) and money-line (two outcomes) markets, from our price feed on Wednesday 25 November, covering the next 40 hours. Youth, reserve and women's competitions are left out because coverage there is patchy.

Best price in the group is how often that bookmaker had the top price (or joint top) of the four, on selections where at least two of them priced it. The events covered: 168 football, 52 ice hockey, 41 basketball, 35 tennis, 5 volleyball, 2 American football, 1 eSports.

How to read it

  • The bookmakers did not all price the same events, so the numbers are a snapshot, not a ranking set in stone. Paddy Power priced the fewest markets in our feed this week.
  • Margins are usually lowest on the biggest games and highest on small leagues and long-odds outcomes.
  • A low average margin does not mean every price is good. Compare the price on the bet you want, or check it against the fair price with our Bet Value Checker.
