Crypto casinos: how to check a licence before you play

Many crypto casinos hold an offshore licence and are not allowed to take players from the UK or most of the US. Here is how to check.

CryptoLicensing and regulation
By the BetMeisters desk16 November 20264 min read
Where it's legalChecked 16 Nov 2026
Most crypto casinos are licensed offshore (for example Curaçao or Anjouan) and exclude players from the UK, the US and other countries in their terms. Playing from a restricted country can mean winnings are withheld. BetMeisters.news only lists operators with a licence we can verify, and shows the countries they accept.

A licence is the first thing to check at any casino, and it matters even more with crypto, where deposits are hard to reverse.

Three checks

  1. Find the licence number in the site footer and check it on the regulator's own website, not just the badge on the casino page.
  2. Read the restricted countries in the terms. If your country is listed, do not play: the casino can refuse to pay out.
  3. Check how disputes work. UK and US state regulators have formal complaint routes. Many offshore licences offer far less protection.

Crypto-specific risks

The value of your coins can change while they sit in a casino balance, withdrawals can be held for checks, and on-chain payments cannot be charged back. Treat a crypto casino deposit as money you can afford to lose entirely.

Every crypto operator we review will show its licence, the regulator, and the countries it accepts and excludes, in that order, before anything else.
