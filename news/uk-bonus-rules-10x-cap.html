UK casino bonuses: the 10x wagering cap explained

Since 19 January 2026 UK bonuses can ask you to re-stake winnings no more than ten times, and bet-plus-casino offers are banned.

Bonuses, rewards and VIPOnline casinoLicensing and regulation
By the BetMeisters desk16 November 20264 min read
Where it's legalChecked 16 Nov 2026
These rules apply to operators licensed by the UK Gambling Commission when they offer bonuses to UK customers. Other regulators set their own rules: an offer with higher wagering may be legal elsewhere, but it cannot be offered to UK players.

Wagering requirements are the number of times you must stake a bonus, or bonus winnings, before you can withdraw. For years many UK casino offers asked for 30x, 40x or even 50x. Since 19 January 2026, the UK Gambling Commission caps this at 10x.

What changed

  • Wagering capped at 10x. A GBP 10 bonus at the old 50x meant GBP 500 of play before a withdrawal; at 10x it is GBP 100.
  • No mixed-product offers. Operators can no longer offer promotions that need you to use more than one product, such as betting and slots, to unlock a bonus.

The Commission's stated aims are less harm, less complexity and clearer terms, while keeping bonuses available.

What it means for you

A UK offer showing more than 10x wagering is either out of date or not aimed at UK players. That is a quick test of whether a comparison site keeps its offers current. On BetMeisters.news we only show an offer where it is legal for the reader, with its key terms beside it, and we re-check every offer's date.

What has not changed

The cap does not make a bonus free money. Maximum bet rules, expiry dates and game contribution still matter, and the house edge still applies to every spin.

A bonus is not a reason to gamble more than you planned. Set a limit before you play.
