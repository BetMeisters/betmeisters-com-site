Why the numbers like a low-scoring night in Fredericia

The Number Cruncher Meister took Under 60.5 goals at 1.95. Here is the price, the chance and the record behind it.

HandballSports betting
By the BetMeisters desk16 November 20263 min read
TipNumber Cruncher1.95Under 60.5 goalsFredericia HK 1990 v Skjern HaandboldDenmark · HandboldligaenFair price 1.76 · edge +10.8%Chance56.8%ResultLostStake1 ptRecord at BetMeisters.com · no affiliate deals · 18+

The Number Cruncher Meister (NCM) does not watch the game and does not have a hunch. It compares every UK bookmaker's price with the sharpest market in the world, Pinnacle, once the bookmaker margin is taken out. When a UK price is clearly bigger than that fair price, it becomes a tip.

For Fredericia v Skjern in the Danish Handboldligaen, Pinnacle's prices made Under 60.5 goals a 56.8% chance, a fair price of 1.76. One UK bookmaker offered 1.95, an edge of about +10.7%. That cleared NCM's bar for handball (10%), so it was published on BetMeisters.com as a Free tip on Sunday at 14:02 EAT.

What has happened since

Markets move. By Monday morning the best UK price had shortened to 1.88, and the edge at that price is closer to 6%. That is normal: when a price is out of line, it usually gets corrected. The tip is recorded at the price we published, 1.95, and it is settled at that price win or lose.

The record behind it

Since 5 November, NCM has published 465 settled tips on BetMeisters.com: 206 won, +83.99 points to level stakes of about one point. That is a test-period record, not a promise. Edges are estimates and plenty of good-value bets lose.
