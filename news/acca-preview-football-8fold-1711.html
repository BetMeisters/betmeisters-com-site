Kyrgyzstan to Cleethorpes: the Acca Meister's 8-fold at 1041.94

Eight football legs from an Asian friendly at 15:00 to five games at 22:45 Kampala time, staked at 0.25 points. It lands about 1 time in 667.

The Acca Meister's long football acca for Tuesday has eight legs and pays 1041.94. It starts with two international friendlies in the afternoon, Kyrgyzstan v Vietnam and Qatar v South Korea, and runs through an Africa Cup of Nations qualifier and the EFL Trophy to four English non-league games. Long accas like this are staked at a quarter of a point.

The 8 legs

  • 15:00 · Kyrgyzstan v Vietnam (International Matches): Kyrgyzstan -0.75 at 3.45. Fair chance about 31.1%. What it needs: Kyrgyzstan must win by 2 goals or more for a full win; a 1-goal win wins half, the other half comes back; a draw or defeat loses.
  • 17:00 · Qatar v Korea Republic (International Matches): South Korea -1 at 1.75. Fair chance about 58.5%. What it needs: South Korea must win by 2 goals or more for a full win; a 1-goal win returns the stake; a draw or defeat loses.
  • 22:00 · Botswana v Uganda (Africa Cup of Nations): Draw at 2.8. Fair chance about 37.5%. What it needs: only a draw will do.
  • 22:00 · Luton Town v Peterborough United (England EFL Trophy): Over 4.5 goals at 3. Fair chance about 34.0%. What it needs: 5 or more goals wins in full; fewer loses.
  • 22:45 · Poland v Sweden (UEFA Nations League): Over 3.75 goals at 3. Fair chance about 36.0%. What it needs: 5 or more goals wins in full; exactly 4 wins half, the other half comes back; fewer loses.
  • 22:45 · Leamington FC v Halesowen Town FC (England Amateur Southern League): Under 3.25 goals at 1.9. Fair chance about 57.6%. What it needs: 2 goals or fewer wins in full; exactly 3 wins half, the other half comes back; more loses.
  • 22:45 · Poole Town v Wimborne Town (England Amateur Southern League): Poole Town +0 at 1.825. Fair chance about 58.7%. What it needs: Poole Town must win; a draw returns the stake; a defeat loses.
  • 22:45 · Cleethorpes Town FC v FC United of Manchester (England Amateur Northern Premier League): FC United of Manchester +0.75 at 1.975. Fair chance about 53.8%. What it needs: a win or a draw for FC United of Manchester wins the leg; a 1-goal defeat loses half, the other half comes back; a 2-goal defeat or worse loses.

The honest numbers

Multiply the fair chances together and the acca lands about 0.1% of the time: roughly 1 in 667, ignoring half-wins and returned stakes. At 1041.94 that is an edge of about +56% on paper. Edge is a long-run idea; over one bet the most likely result is that it loses. The stake is 0.25 points.

Before you copy it

  • Small stakes only. Multiples go long runs without landing.
  • Prices move. If a leg has shortened, the whole price drops with it.
  • Never chase. A losing multiple is the normal result, not a near miss.
