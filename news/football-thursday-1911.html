Meister previews · Thursday 19 November

Thursday's football: Arsenal v OL Lyonnes, an MLS wild card with Griezmann and Cincinnati, and the Brasileirão

The Women's Champions League finishes matchday 5, Orlando City host FC Cincinnati in the MLS Cup playoffs, and Vasco, São Paulo and Vitória play in Brazil. Every main game with its chance on the sharp market.

Thursday's football runs from lunchtime in Asia to the early hours in the Americas. The headline games are the second half of the Women's Champions League matchday 5, with Arsenal v OL Lyonnes the pick at 8pm UK (3pm ET); an MLS Cup playoff wild card in Orlando at 7.30pm ET (00.30 UK); and three Brasileirão games. All times below are UK, with Eastern time for the evening games.

The chances quoted are from Pinnacle, a sharp bookmaker, with the margin removed. They are the market's view, for 90 minutes, taken at about 4pm UK on Wednesday.

Women's Champions League, matchday 5 (Thursday)

Five games, completing a matchday that started on Wednesday. According to UEFA's preview, OL Lyonnes lead the league phase and Arsenal are fourth; holders Barcelona had a perfect start until a 1-1 home draw with Arsenal on matchday 4.

Arsenal v OL Lyonnes · 8pm (3pm ET)

The game of the night. It is the 13th meeting between the clubs, which UEFA notes makes it, with OL Lyonnes v Wolfsburg, the joint-most-played fixture in the competition. Lyonnes won last season's league phase meeting 2-1 at Meadow Park. Arsenal arrive with that draw in Barcelona and a win over Benfica in which Alessia Russo scored a hat-trick.

The market still makes the visitors clear favourites: OL Lyonnes about 54%, the draw about 24%, Arsenal about 22%. It expects goals too: over 2.5 goals is about 62%. Arsenal at home to the leaders at roughly 1 in 4.5 is a big underdog price for a side that has just drawn in Barcelona.

Austria Wien v Bayern München · 5.45pm (12.45pm ET)

The most one-sided game of the night on the numbers. Austria Wien are bottom; Bayern are unbeaten and beat Paris SG 2-1 on matchday 4 to move into the top four. The market makes Bayern about 87%, the draw 10% and Austria Wien about 4%.

The other three

Paris FC v HB Køge (5.45pm) is a big game for the hosts, who are still without a win in the league phase. Barcelona v Servette and Benfica v Roma kick off at 8pm. Benfica are ninth on six points after beating Austria Wien; Roma are 12th after their first league phase win, against Servette. We did not have reliable prices for these three when we wrote this.

MLS Cup playoffs: Orlando City v FC Cincinnati · 7.30pm ET (00.30 UK)

The Eastern Conference wild card, at Inter.co Stadium in Orlando, live on Apple TV. The winner plays Nashville SC, the Supporters' Shield winners, in a best-of-three first-round series.

MLS's own preview sets it up as a goals game. Antoine Griezmann, who joined Orlando from Atlético Madrid in the summer, has 14 goals and 8 assists in 18 appearances, and Orlando won nine of their 13 games after his debut. Martín Ojeda has 16 goals. For Cincinnati, Kévin Denkey has 17 and Evander has a club-best 31 goal contributions (14 goals, 17 assists). Both sides were in the league's top ten for goals scored and for goals conceded, and when they met in May, Cincinnati won 6-2.

The market sees a coin flip in 90 minutes: Orlando about 39%, Cincinnati about 38%, the draw about 24%. The goals line is the striking number: over 2.5 goals about 74%, the highest of any game on Thursday's card.

Brazil: Série A

Vasco da Gama v Internacional (10.30pm UK, 5.30pm ET): Vasco are about 59% at home, Internacional about 18%. Vitória v Red Bull Bragantino (10.30pm) is close to even: Vitória 35%, draw 28%, Bragantino 37%. Late on, São Paulo v Fluminense (00.30 UK Friday, 7.30pm ET Thursday) has São Paulo at about 40% and Fluminense about 31%, with over 2.5 goals only about 45%: the market expects a tight game.

The rest of the Americas

In Uruguay's Clausura, Peñarol are about 66% at home to Cerro Largo (7.30pm UK), and Deportivo Maldonado about 53% away at Boston River. In Colombia, Real Cartagena are about 68% at home to Tigres (00.45 Friday), while Independiente Valle del Cauca v Envigado is priced almost exactly even at 34% each.

Europe and Africa

In France's Ligue 3, Amiens v Caen (7.45pm) has Amiens about 43% and Caen about 28%. The cups in Slovakia and Slovenia have big favourites: Košice about 73% at Zvolen and Celje about 66% at Beltinci. In Algeria, JS Kabylie v USM Alger (7pm) is the pick of Thursday's Algerian Ligue 1 games: Kabylie about 43%, the draw an unusually high 33%, and over 2.5 goals only about 34%.

Asia and Australia

The ASEAN Club Championship has three games around midday UK. Johor Darul Ta'zim are about 52% away at Cong An Ha Noi (12.15pm); PKR Svay Rieng v Port FC (noon) is close (39% v 36%). The most one-sided is Persib Bandung v Shan United (noon): Persib are about 86% to win, and that game carries a BetMeisters tip on the goals line, shown in the box on this page.

Elsewhere: in Japan's WE League, INAC Kobe Leonessa are about 54% at RB Omiya Ardija (9am); in the UAE, Al Ain are about 84% at home to Al Dhafra (3.25pm); and in the Australian Championship, Preston Lions are about 84% against South Hobart (8.30am).
