FPL Gameweek 11: the deadline, the captains the market likes, clean sheets and the transfers everyone is making

The deadline is 11am UK on Saturday 21 November. Manchester City, Tottenham, Brighton and Liverpool have the best team-goals numbers; Brighton and Arsenal the best clean-sheet chances. Every fixture with its odds-based numbers.

FootballSports betting
By the BetMeisters desk18 November 20264 min read

Fantasy Premier League returns after the international break with Gameweek 11. The official FPL site lists the deadline as 11am UK time on Saturday 21 November (6am ET, 2pm in Kampala), 90 minutes before Manchester City v Fulham kicks off. Make your transfers and pick your captain before then.

This preview does something most FPL guides do not: it uses the betting market. Pinnacle, a sharp bookmaker, prices how many goals each team will score. With the margin removed, those prices give the chance of a team scoring two or more (useful for captains and attackers) and the chance of the opponent failing to score (a clean sheet for defenders and goalkeepers). All numbers are from Wednesday afternoon and will move with team news.

The fixtures

  • Saturday 12.30pm: Manchester City v Fulham (City about 68%)
  • Saturday 3pm: Tottenham v Ipswich (Spurs about 62%)
  • Saturday 3pm: Aston Villa v Sunderland (Villa about 52%)
  • Saturday 3pm: Chelsea v Leeds (Chelsea about 51%)
  • Saturday 3pm: Bournemouth v Nottingham Forest (Bournemouth about 50%)
  • Saturday 3pm: Coventry v Crystal Palace (Coventry about 39%, Palace about 35%)
  • Saturday 5.30pm: Newcastle v Arsenal (Arsenal about 61%)
  • Sunday 2pm: Hull v Brighton (Brighton about 64%)
  • Sunday 4.30pm: Liverpool v Manchester United (Liverpool about 55%)
  • Monday 8pm: Brentford v Everton (Brentford about 46%)

Captains: who is most likely to score twice or more?

The single best guide to captaincy is how many goals a player's team is expected to score. Ranked by the market's chance of scoring two or more:

  • Tottenham (home v Ipswich): about 63%
  • Brighton (away at Hull): about 62%
  • Liverpool (home v Manchester United): about 61%
  • Arsenal (away at Newcastle): about 59%
  • Aston Villa (home v Sunderland): about 53%
  • Chelsea (home v Leeds): about 52%
  • Bournemouth (home v Forest): about 50%

Manchester City are priced on a higher line: the market makes them about 42% to score three or more at home to Fulham, which puts them above everyone else in expected goals for the week. That makes City's main attackers the obvious captaincy picks for managers who own them, with Spurs, Brighton and Liverpool players the strongest alternatives. Note the timing: City play in the 12.30pm game, so a City captain's score is known early, before the Saturday 3pm games.

Clean sheets: defenders and goalkeepers

The market's chance of each favourite keeping a clean sheet (the opponent failing to score):

  • Brighton at Hull: about 40%
  • Arsenal at Newcastle: about 39%
  • Manchester City v Fulham: about 38%
  • Tottenham v Ipswich: about 37%
  • Bournemouth v Forest: about 34%
  • Aston Villa v Sunderland: about 33%
  • Chelsea v Leeds: about 32%

No team is better than 40% for a clean sheet this week, which is low for a gameweek that includes several home favourites. In other words, the market expects goals at both ends almost everywhere. The defenders worth owning are the ones who also offer attacking returns.

The transfers managers are making

Fantasy Football Fix's transfer data ahead of Gameweek 11 shows where the FPL crowd is moving:

  • Nicolas Jackson is the most-bought player, with 50 points in his last six gameweeks.
  • Joško Gvardiol to Jan Paul van Hecke is the most popular transfer of all (22.1% of the transfers tracked).
  • João Pedro has been the most-sold player for three weeks running.
  • Thierno Barry scored 16 points in Gameweek 10; his next three fixtures are Brentford, Liverpool and Aston Villa.
  • Evanilson has scored in five consecutive matches.
  • Pascal Groß is one booking away from a suspension.
  • Dominik Szoboszlai made his first league start for Liverpool since Gameweek 5 in Gameweek 10 and created six chances.

The crowd is not always right, but it does move prices, and price rises reward managers who move first.

Team news

Most clubs hold their press conferences on Thursday and Friday, and the FPL site flags injuries and doubts on each player. At the time of writing we have not seen confirmed injury news from a reputable source for this gameweek that we can report, so check the official flags and the pre-match press conferences before the deadline. A late absence can change a captaincy choice more than any fixture.

The short version

Captain: a Manchester City attacker if you have one (about 42% for three or more City goals at home to Fulham), otherwise Spurs, Brighton or Liverpool (each 61% to 63% to score twice). Defence: Brighton and Arsenal have the best clean-sheet numbers, but at about 40% nobody is a banker. Deadline: 11am UK, Saturday 21 November.
