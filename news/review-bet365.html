Our view in one minute

bet365 is the bookmaker most others are measured against, and on our own data it earns that. In our first price scan it had the best UK price on more than half of all main-market selections and the lowest margin of the four big UK firms we track. It also has one of the deepest product sets in betting: streaming, bet builders and best odds guaranteed on racing.

But cheapest is not the same as fair. Its average price was still about 10.7% below the sharp-market fair price. That is the honest gap between the best of the high street and the sharp end of the market, and it is why bet365 scores 7.3, not 9-plus, for its sportsbook.

Who bet365 suits

  • Most UK bettors who want one account that covers almost everything: football, racing, tennis, US sports and niche sports.
  • In-play bettors who watch and bet at the same time: streaming is the best of any UK bookmaker we have looked at.
  • Bet builder fans: up to 12 selections from one match on 15+ sports.

It suits less well if you want the very best price on every bet: no single bookmaker has that, so comparing prices still pays.

Markets and features

One reviewer counted 39 sports. bet365 says it streams more than 600,000 events a year, with a video wall for several games at once. Cash out (full and partial) is offered on many markets, the bet builder covers 15+ sports, and football bets can pay out early when a team goes two goals up. For racing, best odds guaranteed applies to UK and Irish races on win and each-way bets. We have not yet confirmed regular extra-place offers, so racing terms score 7.5 rather than 10.

Payments and withdrawals

Deposits by debit card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, PayPal, bank transfer and paysafecard are listed in published reviews, with small minimums. Reviewers report withdrawals to cards and e-wallets in 1 to 4 hours. We do not score withdrawal speed until we have made our own test withdrawal: that is the single most important check left.

Terms: the small print that decides payouts

Not yet read in full. One part is scored from public reporting: how bet365 treats winning customers (check T4 below). bet365's own website is not reachable from our checking tools, so we have not yet read its full terms. The parts we have not read are left out of the score until we do, which means they count neither for nor against bet365.

When we read them we will report, in plain English and good or bad: caps on what one bet or one day can pay; how wide the right is to void bets or keep winnings; and how winning customers are treated, including stake limits. Until then, read the terms yourself before you deposit, especially the maximum-winnings section.

Licence and safety

bet365 holds a UK Gambling Commission licence (account 55148, Hillside (UK Sports) ENC), active since October 2019, and bet365.com is listed on the register. In April 2024 it paid GBP 239,085 in lieu of a penalty over anti-money-laundering and customer-interaction failings in 2021-22. That is why its licence record scores 5 out of 10, the main thing holding its core score below a perfect 10.

Safer gambling tools

The tool set is one of the most complete we have seen: deposit and stake limits, reality checks, a monthly budget calculator, time-outs from 24 hours to 30 days and self-exclusion from six months to five years or indefinitely. As a UK licensee it must also honour GAMSTOP. It scores 10.

Support and apps

Live chat runs around the clock but starts with a bot; one reviewer waited about 20 minutes for a person (source). There is email and a webform, but not a phone line in every region, so support scores 7.5 until our own chat test. The apps are very highly rated: around 4.7-4.8 on iOS and 4.5-4.6 on Android from hundreds of thousands of ratings.

FAQs

Is bet365 legit? Yes. It is licensed by the UK Gambling Commission (account 55148) and we have checked its domain on the regulator's register.

Does bet365 have the best odds? Of the four big UK bookmakers we track, it had the best price most often in our first scan (56% of selections) and the lowest margin. No bookmaker is best on every bet.

How fast are bet365 withdrawals? Published reviews say 1 to 4 hours to cards and e-wallets. We will confirm with our own test.

Does bet365 offer best odds guaranteed? Yes, on UK and Irish horse racing, win and each-way.

Can I stream sport on bet365? Yes: it streams more than 600,000 events a year, usually for customers with a funded account or a bet placed.
