The early game is the most open: Aston Villa are 43% in Istanbul against Galatasaray's 33%, and the market expects goals, at 72% for three or more. England and Scotland

A full Championship programme starts at 7.45pm. West Ham are 52% at Portsmouth, Cardiff 63% at home to Preston (67% for three or more goals) and Middlesbrough 67% against Swansea. Sheffield United v Southampton is a coin flip at 38% against 37%, and Wolves are 73% at home to Derby (8pm). In League One, Leicester are 56% at Blackpool. In Scotland, Rangers are 79% at home to Aberdeen and Hibernian 59% against Dundee.

Asia: the AFC Champions League

The AFC Champions League Elite starts early, UK time. Kashiwa Reysol are 68% at Newcastle Jets (7.45am), Daejeon 63% at home to Beijing Guoan and Kyoto Sanga 59% against Pohang (both 10am), and Vissel Kobe 53% in Hanoi (12.15pm). In the evening, Al Nassr are 76% at home to Al Sadd (6.15pm).

How to read these numbers

A chance is not a tip. A 70% favourite fails to win three times in ten. A price only has value when it is bigger than the fair price: for a 43% chance, anything above about 2.33.
