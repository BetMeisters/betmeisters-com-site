Tuesday night in the US: Celtics in Washington, Nuggets v Mavericks, Oilers v Golden Knights and Gonzaga v Kansas State

Three NBA games, the only NHL game in our feed, and a big college basketball card with Baylor v Alabama, Texas Tech v Louisville and Gonzaga v Kansas State. The sharp market's chances with the margin removed, in UK and US Eastern times.

Every chance below is Pinnacle's price with the bookmaker margin removed, taken from our price scan at about 6pm UK on Tuesday. Times are UK first, with US Eastern Time (ET) in brackets. A spread or total is the line where the two sides are closest to 50-50, which is how the sharp market sets it. These are the games in our price feed; a few others are on tonight.

NBA

  • Orlando Magic v Detroit Pistons, midnight (7pm ET): close, with Detroit 55.5% and Orlando 44.5%. Pistons by about 2.5, total 213.
  • Washington Wizards v Boston Celtics, 1am (8pm ET): Boston 78.7%, about 11-point favourites, total 219.5.
  • Denver Nuggets v Dallas Mavericks, 3am (10pm ET): Denver 72.6%, Nuggets −8.5, and the highest total of the night at 234.5.

NHL

Edmonton Oilers v Vegas Golden Knights, 1am (8pm ET), is almost a coin flip: Vegas 52.5%, Edmonton 47.5%, including overtime and shootouts. The total is 6.5 goals. Edmonton +1.5 goals is a 70% chance.

College basketball: the main games

  • Baylor v Alabama, 3am (10pm ET): Alabama 53.4%, a near pick'em, with the biggest total on the card at 174.5.
  • Texas Tech v Louisville, 1am (8pm ET): Texas Tech 55.9%, about 2.5-point favourites, total 153.5.
  • Clemson v Washington, 1am (8pm ET): Clemson 52.9%, total 150.5.
  • Ohio State v Vanderbilt, midnight (7pm ET): Vanderbilt 54.0%, total 157.
  • Purdue v DePaul, 11pm (6pm ET): Purdue 57.7%, about 3-point favourites, total 132.5, the lowest of the main games.
  • VCU v Colorado State, 3.30am (10.30pm ET): VCU 67.9%, about 5.5-point favourites.
  • Oregon v St. John's, 3.30am (10.30pm ET): St. John's about 10.5-point favourites, total 155.5.
  • Colorado v Stanford, 5am (midnight ET): Stanford 61.8%, about 4-point favourites.
  • Gonzaga v Kansas State, 5.30am (12.30am ET): Gonzaga 93.0%, about 16-point favourites.

Several of the big names have mismatches tonight: Florida about −34 against Milwaukee, Villanova −33.5 against Coppin State, Missouri −30 against Evansville and Indiana −28 against Jacksonville. Spreads that big are common in November college basketball, and bench minutes late in a blowout can decide them, so treat them with care.
