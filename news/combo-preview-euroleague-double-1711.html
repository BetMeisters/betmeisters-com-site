  • 19:00 · Dubai Basketball v Anadolu Efes (Euroleague): Dubai Basketball -7.5 at 1.76. Fair chance about 52.8%. What it needs: Dubai Basketball must win by 8 points or more; overtime counts.

The honest numbers

Multiply the fair chances together and the combo lands about 26.9% of the time: roughly 1 in 4. The stake is 1 point.

Before you copy it

  • Small stakes only. Multiples go long runs without landing.
  • Prices move. If a leg has shortened, the whole price drops with it.
  • Never chase. A losing multiple is the normal result, not a near miss.
