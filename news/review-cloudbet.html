Our view in one minute

Cloudbet is one of the oldest crypto sportsbooks, and its terms are among the best written we have read from an offshore operator: dated, structured, with material changes emailed and needing your active re-acceptance, and an independent dispute route through CADRE whose decisions bind both sides. It also openly allows betting through its API. The weak points are money ones: a handling charge of up to 5% on deposits and withdrawals, a USD 5 monthly fee on dormant accounts, and a rule that you must wager your deposits in full before withdrawing. Its Curaçao licence is listed as "Assessment in progress": the licence period ended on 23 May 2026 and renewal had not been decided at 20 November.

Licence and where it applies

Licence: Curaçao Gaming Authority, licence OGL/2024/328/0599, held by Halcyon Super Holdings B.V. (company 148526), Willemstad. Register of 20 November 2026: issued 23 November 2025, licence period ended 23 May 2026, status "Assessment in progress". (Cloudbet's own terms give the original issue date as 27 May 2025; the register says 23 May 2025.) Payments are partly handled by Solas Technologies Limited in Cyprus, which is not itself a gambling licensee.

Where you cannot play: you must not be physically in Australia, Austria, Belgium, China (including Macau and Hong Kong), Curaçao, Cuba, France, Germany, Iran, Japan, Lithuania, Malta, Myanmar, the Netherlands, North Korea, Singapore, Syria, Spain, Turkey, the United States, the United Kingdom or the occupied regions of Ukraine, or anywhere else online gambling is unlawful (7.1.3). Bets placed from those places can be voided (10.20).

Payments and withdrawals

  • Coins: around 29 cryptocurrencies, from BTC and ETH to stablecoins, plus fiat where available (7.3.1).
  • Fees: Cloudbet may apply a handling charge of up to 5% on deposits and withdrawals (7.1.10). That is a real cost to a bettor: on a 2.00 bet, 5% each way would wipe out any edge.
  • Play-through: before you withdraw you must wager at least the total of your deposits, though Cloudbet may reduce this (8.8).
  • Speed: crypto withdrawals typically within 24 hours of approval (7.5.11).
  • Identity checks: required once your total deposits reach USD 2,200, via the verification service Sumsub (21.1.3, 21.1.5).
  • Dormant accounts: after 12 months with no activity, and after Cloudbet has tried to contact you, a USD 5 monthly fee can apply until the balance reaches zero (7.1.11).

The sportsbook

Results are settled on the official result and become final 72 hours after posting (10.17, 10.21). Postponed matches not completed by the end of the next calendar day are void, except markets already decided (10.19). Bets above the automatic acceptance level are reviewed by hand, and any not approved before the start are void (13.2). The maximum payout on any parlay or accumulator is EUR 1,000,000 (13.4). If the price moves in your favour between submitting and acceptance, you get the better price (10.7), a fair touch. We have not yet measured Cloudbet's prices against the sharp market, so value is not scored.

Terms: the good and the bad

  • Good: material changes are emailed, name the clauses changed, and need your re-acceptance; they never apply retrospectively to your bets (2.2).
  • Good: an independent dispute service, CADRE, whose decision is binding (22.1, 22.2).
  • Good: the betting API is allowed, so automated bettors are not automatically in breach (5.6).
  • Bad: broad rights to suspend, cancel bets and withhold winnings if Cloudbet merely suspects a breach (11.1).
  • Bad: the Pulse social feature can publish your username, winnings and betting stats, and you grant Cloudbet a wide licence to use them in marketing unless you opt out (14.4, 14.5, 14.11).

FAQs

Can I bet with Cloudbet from the UK? No. The UK is on its excluded list and its Curaçao licence does not cover UK players.

Does Cloudbet charge fees? It may charge up to 5% on deposits and withdrawals, plus USD 5 a month on dormant accounts.

Is its licence valid? The Curaçao register of 20 November 2026 lists it as "Assessment in progress": the licence period ended on 23 May 2026 and renewal had not been decided at that date.
