Which UK bookmaker has the best odds? Our daily margin index

We measure every major UK bookmaker's margin from our own price scans: 18 bookmakers ranked on football, plus NFL, NBA and NHL. No affiliate links, no paid placings, just the numbers, refreshed every day.

Every bookmaker builds a margin into its prices. The smaller the margin, the more of your money comes back over time. We collect prices from about 20 UK bookmakers several times a day for our own tips, so we can measure each one's margin directly. This page is that measurement, refreshed every day. What the gap is worth

On 100 bets of £10 at fair-ish prices, the margin is roughly what you hand over. At Coral's 5.8% that is about £55; at Betano's 13.7% it is about £120. Same bets, same results, £65 difference. Over a season of betting, the bookmaker you use matters as much as the bets you pick.

Bookmakers that share prices

Several UK brands belong to the same group and often show the same or very similar prices: Coral and Ladbrokes (Entain); Paddy Power, Sky Bet and Betfair Sportsbook (Flutter); William Hill and 888sport (Evoke); Unibet, LeoVegas, Grosvenor, Casumo, Virgin Bet and LiveScore Bet use Kambi's trading. Having accounts with two brands in the same group adds less than having accounts in different groups.

How we measure it

  • Prices come from The Odds API's UK feed, collected during our tipping passes; every snapshot is stored.
  • For each event we take each bookmaker's latest full set of prices and work out its margin: the sum of 1 ÷ price, minus 1.
  • Same-match comparison: each bookmaker is measured against the median margin of the other bookmakers on the same matches, then placed on a common scale. Bookmakers that price only the biggest games, where margins are lowest, are not flattered.
  • We count an event only if at least five UK bookmakers priced it, and a bookmaker only if it priced at least five events.
  • The table uses a rolling 30 days. Exchanges are left out (they charge commission instead of a margin).
  • Margins vary by league, market and time to kick-off: popular matches usually carry smaller margins than obscure ones, and side markets carry far bigger margins than the match result.

Our margin guide explains the maths, and every BetMeisters tip shows the fair price with the margin removed, on the tips page.

Common questions

Which UK bookmaker has the best odds? On our latest football data, Coral, with an average margin of 5.8%. The order changes over time, which is why this page is refreshed daily.

Is the bookmaker with the lowest margin always best? On price, yes. But limits, account restrictions, payment speed and terms also matter; our reviews cover those.

Why do some bookmakers have the same margin? Brands in the same group often share one pricing team.
