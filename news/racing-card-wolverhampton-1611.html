Wolverhampton tonight: the nine-race card, and where the favourites are short

Monday evening on the all-weather: two Appleby hotpots in the novices, a busy handicap at 7pm and an evens favourite to close. Here is what the market says, race by race.

Wolverhampton's Tapeta track hosts a nine-race card tonight, from 4.30pm to 8.30pm UK time (7.30pm to 11.30pm in Kampala). The going is standard. Every race is restricted to apprentice or conditional-type riders, so many of the jockeys carry claims. Below, for each race, we show the favourites and what the betting makes their chance once the bookmakers' margin is taken out.

A quick reminder of how to read the numbers: a horse at 2.00 (evens) needs to win half the time to be fair value. If the market makes it 46%, evens is slightly short. If it makes it 55%, evens is generous.

4.30 · Class 5 nursery handicap, 6 furlongs (9 runners)

A wide-open start. Lord Ragnar (Ed Dunlop) and Go Go Girl (Clive Cox) are both around 16% in the market, with Harry's Pop close behind. Nobody is shorter than about 4/1. When a race is this level, small price differences between bookmakers matter more than usual.

5.00 · Class 4 fillies' novice, 6 furlongs (13 runners)

Desert Romance (Charlie Appleby) is the clear favourite around 4/5, and our fair line makes her about a 52% chance, so the best price of 1.83 is a touch short of fair. Threelittleangels (Roger Teal) is the danger at around 5/2. Desert Romance is also a leg in one of the Original Combo Meister's trebles tonight.

5.30 · Class 3 novice, 8½ furlongs (9 runners)

The shortest price of the night: Leading Role, another Appleby runner, at around 3/10. The market makes him about 72%, which means 1.30 is fair at best. Two John and Thady Gosden runners, Afif and Ever Since, are next in at 7/1 and 8/1.

6.00 · Class 3 novice, 8½ furlongs (9 runners)

Al Lahbab (Appleby again) is favourite around 8/11 to 4/6. Our fair line makes him about 55%, so the prices around 1.73 are close to fair. Kings Medal (Gosden) is second favourite around 7/2 to 4/1. Al Lahbab is a leg in the Original Combo Meister's double tonight.

6.30 · Class 3 handicap, 5 furlongs (11 runners)

A competitive sprint with no clear favourite: Knebworth, Tan Rapido and Atomic Force are all between 4/1 and 13/2. Five-furlong sprints at Wolverhampton often turn on the draw and the break.

7.00 · Class 5 handicap, 7 furlongs (11 runners)

Newsreader (Tony Carroll) heads the market around 3/1, with Intervention (Michael Appleby) next around 7/2. Intervention is The Original Meister's pick tonight at 4.00, shown in the box above with its stats. The Original Meister's picks are fully manual and on the public record at BetMeisters.com, win or lose.

7.30 · Class 5 handicap, 8½ furlongs (12 runners)

R P McMurphy (Michael Appleby) is a narrow favourite around 4/1 in a big, open handicap. The market makes nobody better than about 17%.

8.00 · Class 5 handicap, 9½ furlongs (12 runners)

Ted Le Saux (Andrew Balding) is favourite around 2/1 to 9/4. Our fair line makes him about 29%, which is nearer 5/2, so the shorter prices look a little skinny. Sea The Light and Felix Gem are next at 6/1 and 13/2.

8.30 · Class 6 handicap, 1m4f (11 runners)

Saxophonic (David Simcock) closes the card as an evens favourite. He has won two of his last three, and the market makes him about 47%, so evens is about fair rather than value. Havachoc is next at 7/1.

What the numbers say overall

Tonight's card has four strong favourites (Desert Romance, Leading Role, Al Lahbab and Saxophonic) and all four are priced at or slightly under their fair chance. That is normal on all-weather evening cards, where the favourites are well found. The open handicaps at 4.30, 6.30 and 7.30 are where prices differ most between bookmakers, so it pays to compare.
