Our view in one minute

Videoslots has one of the biggest game lobbies in the UK: more than 10,000 games, 750+ live tables and 200+ jackpot slots. If choice is what you want, few casinos come close. But two things hold its score back. Its licence holder was given a £650,000 penalty, a warning and an extra licence condition by the Gambling Commission in October 2025, and on the published evidence its support, apps and withdrawal fees lag behind the best UK casinos.

Who Videoslots suits

  • Players who want the widest choice: 10,300+ games, from classic slots to exclusives.
  • Live casino players: 750+ live tables from five studios, including VIP tables.
  • Jackpot hunters: 200+ jackpot slots across Jackpot King, Mega Moolah and WowPot.

Who should look elsewhere: Android users (there is no Android app), players who withdraw several times a day, and anyone who wants quick, human support.

Games: the lobby

A published review counts more than 10,300 games, and Videoslots' owner says the figure is now over 14,000, which makes it one of the largest lobbies in the UK market. The live casino has 750+ tables from Evolution, Playtech, Pragmatic Play Live, OnAir and Stakelogic. There are 200+ jackpot slots, and community features such as Battle of Slots tournaments. The trade-off is navigation: the same review found the site dated and hard to search with so many games.

Not yet checked: which RTP version Videoslots runs on popular slots. Many slots come in several versions with different returns, and casinos choose which one to offer. We will check ten popular slots ourselves; until then that part is left out of the score.

The welcome offer: check it carefully

The review we read describes a 100% deposit match up to £50 with 10x wagering, plus 11 free spins on Starburst with no wagering. But the terms shown on the same page describe a 100% match up to £200 with 20x wagering. Both cannot be right, so check the current offer on the Videoslots site before you deposit. Since January 2026, UK bonus wagering is capped at 10 times the bonus; our guide to the 10x cap explains what that means.

Payments and withdrawals

Videoslots takes debit cards, Skrill, Neteller, ecoPayz, Instadebit, bank transfer and SMSVoucher; PayPal is not listed. A published review reports most withdrawals processed within 12 hours and bank transfers within two days. The first withdrawal each day is free, but each further withdrawal that day costs £2.50, which counts against it. Deposits are blocked until your documents are verified, which took about 24 hours in one test. We will time a real withdrawal ourselves before scoring speed.

Terms: the small print that decides payouts

We have read Videoslots' terms and conditions. One long document covers the casino, sportsbook, tournaments and bonuses, with no plain-English summary. What matters most for a player:

  • Winnings caps: we found no cap on casino winnings outside bonuses. Sports bets are capped at €100,000 net per bet or combination, lower on some markets. Jackpot wins are paid after a full verification check.
  • Broad rights to keep funds: Videoslots can cancel, reverse or forfeit funds for any breach of its terms, game faults or a "manifest error" (3.8). This is wider than most UK operators we have reviewed.
  • Bonuses: one bonus per household or IP address; suspected bonus abuse means the bonus is forfeited and the account frozen (3.7). Maximum bet with an active bonus is 50% of the bonus or £20, whichever is lower (4.2.2).
  • Fees: bank wire withdrawals are free, but other withdrawals can carry a processing fee shown before you confirm. Deposits withdrawn without being played through can be charged up to 3.95% (2.13). An account unused for 12 months is charged £1 a month if Videoslots cannot return the balance to you (1.17).
  • Withdrawals: minimum £20, with no monthly cap stated (2.8).
  • Your money: held separately from company funds and rated Medium protection (2.5).
  • Disputes: UK players can go to IBAS for free; its decision binds Videoslots for disputes under £10,000 (9).

Overall: no casino winnings cap is a plus, but the rights to forfeit funds and the fees are broader than most rivals.

Licence and safety

Videoslots runs under Videoslots Limited, Gambling Commission account 39380, based in Malta and part of Immense Group, which also runs Mr Vegas. Its remote licence covers casino (since 2014), betting on real events (since 2020) and bingo (since 2025), and www.videoslots.com and www.videoslots.co.uk are listed on it.

The register records one action. On 30 October 2025 the Commission imposed a £650,000 financial penalty, a formal warning and an extra condition on the licence, after finding that between October 2023 and February 2024 Videoslots breached anti-money-laundering rules (licence condition 12.1.1) and failed to meet customer-interaction requirements (Social Responsibility Code 3.4.3). The Commission noted that the business co-operated and took immediate steps to fix the issues. It still counts against Videoslots in our score.

Safer gambling and support

Videoslots prompts you to set a deposit limit at sign-up and also offers loss limits, maximum-bet limits, time-outs and self-exclusion, with GAMSTOP for UK customers. UK online slots are limited to £5 a spin (£2 for 18 to 24 year olds). Support is the weak point on the published evidence: live chat starts with an AI assistant, and one reviewer waited about 30 minutes for a person. Email and a contact form are also available; no phone line is listed.
