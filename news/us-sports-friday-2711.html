Black Friday in the US: Broncos at Steelers, Texas at Texas A&M and a full NBA card

One NFL game, the college football rivalry games, 11 NBA games, a busy NHL night and big college basketball. The sharp market's chances with the margin removed, in UK and US Eastern times, plus Saturday's early BetMeisters Free tips.

Every chance below is Pinnacle's price with the bookmaker margin removed, taken from our price scan at about 6pm UK on Friday. Times are UK first, with US Eastern Time (ET) in brackets; most of the night runs into the early hours of Saturday in the UK. A spread or total is the line where the two sides are closest to 50-50. Games where BetMeisters has a Pro tip or a combo leg are left out.

NFL

Denver Broncos at Pittsburgh Steelers, 8pm (3pm ET) at Acrisure Stadium: Denver are 69.3% to win away from home, about 4.5-point favourites, with a low total of 37 points. Overtime counts for the winner, spread and total markets.

College football: rivalry week

Nebraska at Iowa and Mississippi State at Ole Miss were already under way as this was published.

  • App State at South Alabama, 8pm (3pm ET): South Alabama 58.9%, 3-point favourites, total 57.
  • Florida at Florida State, 8.30pm (3.30pm ET): Florida 69.5% on the road, about 6.5-point favourites, total 54.5.
  • Texas at Texas A&M, 12.30am (7.30pm ET) at Kyle Field: Texas 80.0%, 11-point favourites, total 41.5.
  • Minnesota at Wisconsin, 12.30am (7.30pm ET): Wisconsin 75.5%, 9.5-point favourites, and the lowest total of the night at 37.5.
  • West Virginia at Utah, 2am (9pm ET): Utah 93.9%, 26-point favourites, total 65.5.

NBA

  • Miami Heat at New York Knicks, midnight (7pm ET): Knicks 60.2%, 3-point favourites, total 222.5.
  • Milwaukee Bucks at Detroit Pistons, 12.30am (7.30pm ET): Detroit 78.8%, 10-point favourites.
  • Philadelphia 76ers at Indiana Pacers, 12.30am (7.30pm ET): Philadelphia 61.2% away, about 3.5-point favourites, total 230.
  • Atlanta Hawks at Boston Celtics, 1am (8pm ET): Boston 77.1%, 8.5-point favourites.
  • Memphis Grizzlies at Minnesota Timberwolves, 1.30am (8.30pm ET): Minnesota 61.7%, 4-point favourites.
  • Dallas Mavericks at Phoenix Suns, 2am (9pm ET): close, Phoenix 53.1%, total 227.5.
  • Los Angeles Lakers at San Antonio Spurs, 2.30am (9.30pm ET): San Antonio 67.4%, 6.5-point favourites.
  • Denver Nuggets at Utah Jazz, 2.30am (9.30pm ET): Denver 78.8%, 9.5-point favourites, total 230.5.
  • Golden State Warriors at Portland Trail Blazers, 2.30am (9.30pm ET): Portland 78.5%, 10-point favourites.
  • New Orleans Pelicans at LA Clippers, 2.30am (9.30pm ET): Clippers 55.4%, 2.5-point favourites, total 230.5.

NHL

Three NHL games had already started at 6pm UK. Five of the later ones:

  • Florida Panthers at Washington Capitals, 8pm (3pm ET): Florida 52.9%; Under 6.5 goals 48.4%.
  • Colorado Avalanche at Minnesota Wild, 9pm (4pm ET): Minnesota 51.3%; Under 6.5 goals 54.3%.
  • Winnipeg Jets at New York Islanders, 12.30am (7.30pm ET): Islanders 52.9%.
  • Pittsburgh Penguins at Buffalo Sabres, 1am (8pm ET): Buffalo 54.7%.
  • Seattle Kraken at Edmonton Oilers, 1am (8pm ET): Edmonton 65.8%.

Win chances include overtime and the shootout.

College basketball

Most of these are neutral-site tournament games, so the first-named team is not necessarily the away side.

  • Villanova at Kansas, 10pm (5pm ET) at Allen Fieldhouse: Kansas 85.3%, 10.5-point favourites.
  • Marquette v Texas A&M, 10pm (5pm ET): close, Marquette 53.2%.
  • Michigan v Kansas State, 10.30pm (5.30pm ET): Michigan 80.0%, 9.5-point favourites, total 165.5.
  • West Virginia v North Carolina, midnight (7pm ET) in Charlotte: West Virginia 61.1%, 3.5-point favourites.
  • Miami v Alabama, 1.30am (8.30pm ET): Miami 55.5%, and the highest total on the card at 170.
  • Tennessee v Louisville, 2am (9pm ET): Louisville 58.7%, 3.5-point favourites.
  • Wichita State at Kentucky, 2am (9pm ET): Kentucky 65.2%, 4.5-point favourites.
  • Creighton v Gonzaga, 4am (11pm ET): Gonzaga 69.9%, 5.5-point favourites.
  • San Diego State v Texas Tech, 4.30am (11.30pm ET): Texas Tech 77.1%, 8.5-point favourites.

  • Under 5.5 goals in Blackhawks v Rangers, 10.30pm (5.30pm ET), at 2.30 (overtime and shootout count). The sharp market makes it 44.6%, a fair price of about 2.24.
