Comoros, Tonbridge, Leamington and Poole: the Acca Meister's Tuesday football 4-fold at 20.42

One AFCON draw and three English non-league legs at 7.45pm UK time. Every leg is priced above its fair chance, but the acca lands about 1 time in 15.

The Acca Meister's Tuesday football acca mixes an Africa Cup of Nations qualifier with three games in English non-league football, combined into a 4-fold at 20.42. It only uses legs where the price is above the fair chance on the sharp market, with the bookmaker margin removed.

The four legs

  • 22:00 · Congo Republic v Comoros (Africa Cup of Nations): Draw at 3.1. Fair chance about 35.3%.
  • 22:45 · Tonbridge Angels v Chesham United (England Amateur National League South): Tonbridge Angels to win at 1.9. Fair chance about 54.8%.
  • 22:45 · Leamington FC v Halesowen Town FC (England Amateur Southern League): Under 3.25 goals at 1.9. Fair chance about 57.6%.
  • 22:45 · Poole Town v Wimborne Town (England Amateur Southern League): Poole Town +0 at 1.825. Fair chance about 58.7%.

What each leg needs

Congo Republic v Comoros, draw. Only a draw will do. The market makes Congo Republic slight favourites at home, with the draw about a 35% shot. The same draw is also a leg of the Acca Meister's four-draw AFCON acca.

Tonbridge Angels to win against Chesham United in the National League South. A straight win; a draw loses.

Leamington v Halesowen, Under 3.25 goals. A quarter line: two goals or fewer wins in full, exactly three goals wins half the stake and returns the other half, and four or more loses. It is also one of the Number Cruncher Meister's singles on BetMeisters.com.

Poole Town +0 (draw no bet) at home to Wimborne Town. A Poole win wins, a draw returns that leg's stake (the acca then pays on the other three legs), and a Wimborne win loses.

The honest numbers

Multiply the four fair chances together and the acca lands about 6.5% of the time: roughly 1 in 15, ignoring the half-win and push outcomes. At 20.42 that is an edge of about +34% on paper. Edge is a long-run idea; over one bet the most likely result is that it loses. That is why the Acca Meister stakes it at 0.5 points.

How to think about accas

  • Small stakes only. Long-odds accas go long runs without landing.
  • Each leg should be value on its own. A leg priced below its fair chance drags the whole acca down.
  • Never chase. A losing acca is the normal result, not a near miss.
