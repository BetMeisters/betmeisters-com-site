Two AFCON draws, Bonaire and goals in Copenhagen: the Acca Meister's 5-fold at 261.6

Five legs from 16:00 to 22:45 Kampala time, all priced above their fair chance. It lands about 1 time in 196.

A 5-fold at 261.6 built at 11:03 Kampala time. Two legs are Africa Cup of Nations qualifier draws, one is Bonaire not to lose in the CONCACAF Nations League, one is Cape Verde to win well in Liberia, and the last is over 4 goals in Denmark v Norway in the Nations League. Every leg is an underdog-priced selection, so the acca is very unlikely to land; the point is that each price was above the fair chance.

The 5 legs

  • 16:00 · Ethiopia v Mozambique (Africa Cup of Nations): Draw at 2.9. Fair chance about 38.0%. What it needs: only a draw will do.
  • 22:00 · Saint Kitts and Nevis v Bonaire (CONCACAF Nations League): Bonaire -0 at 3.55. Fair chance about 30.3%. What it needs: Bonaire must win; a draw returns the stake; a defeat loses.
  • 22:00 · Botswana v Uganda (Africa Cup of Nations): Draw at 2.8. Fair chance about 37.5%. What it needs: only a draw will do.
  • 22:00 · Liberia v Cape Verde (Africa Cup of Nations): Cape Verde -1.25 at 3.3. Fair chance about 31.2%. What it needs: Cape Verde must win by 2 goals or more for a full win; a 1-goal win loses half, the other half comes back; a draw or defeat loses.
  • 22:45 · Denmark v Norway (UEFA Nations League): Over 4 goals at 2.75. Fair chance about 37.5%. What it needs: 5 or more goals wins in full; exactly 4 returns the stake; fewer loses.

The honest numbers

Multiply the fair chances together and the acca lands about 0.5% of the time: roughly 1 in 196, ignoring half-wins and returned stakes. At 261.6 that is an edge of about +33% on paper. Edge is a long-run idea; over one bet the most likely result is that it loses. The stake is 0.5 points.

Before you copy it

  • Small stakes only. Multiples go long runs without landing.
  • Prices move. If a leg has shortened, the whole price drops with it.
  • Never chase. A losing multiple is the normal result, not a near miss.
