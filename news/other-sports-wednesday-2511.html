Wednesday away from football: Real Madrid host Zalgiris, a full Liiga night and Italy in the Davis Cup

Real Madrid and Dubai are clear EuroLeague favourites, the UK Elite League has two Scottish home games, Italy are 90% against Korea in the Davis Cup and the CEV Cup fills the volleyball evening. Every main game with its chance on the sharp market.

Champions League football takes the headlines again tonight, but there is plenty elsewhere. All times are UK; the chances are from the sharp market with the bookmaker margin removed, taken at midday on Wednesday. Games where BetMeisters has an open tip are left out.

Basketball: the EuroLeague

  • Dubai v Partizan, 4pm: Dubai 73.0%.
  • Real Madrid v Zalgiris Kaunas, 7.45pm: Real Madrid 73.8%, the clearest favourite of the night.

EuroLeague money lines include overtime. Late team news matters more in basketball than in most sports, so check the starting line-ups before you bet. In the women's EuroLeague, USK Prague are 95.4% against AZS Lublin (6pm) and Schio 63.2% away at Crvena zvezda (5pm).

Ice hockey: the UK Elite League, Liiga and the ICE League

  • UK Elite League, both at 7.30pm: Nottingham Panthers 75.5% at Dundee Stars, and Coventry Blaze 52.8% at Glasgow Clan, close to a coin toss.
  • Finland, Liiga, all at 4.30pm: Jokerit 66.3% against KooKoo, Karpat 62.4% against Pelicans and HIFK 59.4% against TPS.
  • ICE Hockey League: Bolzano 60.3% against KAC Klagenfurt, 6.45pm.
  • Sweden, HockeyAllsvenskan: Almtuna 70.0% against Visby/Roma, 6pm.

These chances are for the team to win including overtime and shootouts. Many bookmakers' default hockey market is the three-way result after 60 minutes, which is a different bet with a draw, so check which one you are backing.

Tennis: the Davis Cup and WTA 125 Buenos Aires

  • Davis Cup Finals: Italy 89.9% against South Korea, from 3pm.
  • WTA 125, Buenos Aires: Martina Trevisan 57.0% against Veronika Erjavec at 6pm, and Solana Sierra 58.0% against Carole Monnet at 9pm.
  • Challenger tennis runs in Florianopolis and San Salvador. These are lower-profile events where prices can move more and late withdrawals happen: check how your bookmaker settles a retirement.

Volleyball: the CEV Cup and Spain's Superliga

  • CEV Cup (women): MTV Stuttgart 91.1% at KNRC (5pm), Gran Canaria 87.8% against Oudegem (7pm), and FC Porto 55.4% against Zok Ub (8pm), the closest of the night.
  • Spain, Superliga: six games from 5pm, with CV Manacor 84.2% against Leganes the biggest favourite.

BetMeisters has no tips in these games. Today's tips, all free to read until 1 January, are on the tips page.
