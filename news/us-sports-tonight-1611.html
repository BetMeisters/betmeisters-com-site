US sports overnight: Ravens v Chargers on Monday Night Football, eight NBA games and a big college slate

Monday night in the US runs through the Kampala small hours. The sharp market's spreads and totals for the NFL, NBA, NHL and college basketball, plus the two BetMeisters tips running.

The US sports night starts as Kampala goes to bed. College basketball tips off from 2am, the NBA from 3am, the NHL from 3.30am, and Monday Night Football kicks off at 4.15am. Below is what the sharpest market makes of the main games, with the bookmaker margin removed. US betting works mostly on spreads (a points handicap) and totals (over or under a number of points), so that is what we show. A spread priced at about 50% on each side means the market thinks the line is set right.

NFL · Monday Night Football

Baltimore Ravens v Los Angeles Chargers · 04:15

The Ravens are 4.5-point favourites at home, and the market is split almost exactly down the middle on that line: 50.2% for Baltimore to cover, 49.8% for the Chargers. The total is low at 39 points, with the over at 50.7%. A total that low says the market expects defences to have the upper hand. When a spread sits on 4.5, the key numbers in American football (3 and 7, the value of a field goal and a converted touchdown) matter: a Ravens win by exactly 4 or by 3 loses on the spread, and a win by 5 or more covers.

NBA · eight games

Eight NBA games run from 3am to 5am Kampala time. On every one of them the main spread is priced close to 50/50, which is normal: NBA lines are among the most efficient in betting.

  • Boston Celtics v Orlando Magic (03:00): Boston −8.5, total 212.5, the lowest of the night. Boston to cover 48.8%.
  • Cleveland Cavaliers v Chicago Bulls (03:00): Cleveland −9, and the highest total on the board at 241.5. That is the market expecting a fast, open game.
  • Washington Wizards v Memphis Grizzlies (03:00): Memphis −3.5 away, total 232.5.
  • Brooklyn Nets v Philadelphia 76ers (03:30): Philadelphia −4.5 away, total 225.5, with the over slightly favoured at 50.9%.
  • Miami Heat v Houston Rockets (03:30): Houston −4.5 away, total 232.
  • Minnesota Timberwolves v Portland Trail Blazers (04:00): Minnesota only −2 at home, total 227.5. The Original Meister has Portland to win at 2.20, a fully manual pick, published this morning. At −2 the market makes this close to a coin flip; when the pick was published the sharp market had Portland at about 44.4% to win, against 45.5% implied by the 2.20.
  • New Orleans Pelicans v Golden State Warriors (04:00): Golden State −3.5 away, total 224.
  • Denver Nuggets v Phoenix Suns (05:00): Denver −9.5, the biggest NBA favourite of the night, total 230.

NHL

New York Rangers v Montreal Canadiens (03:30): Montreal are favourites away from home. The puck line (a 1.5-goal handicap) has the Rangers +1.5 at about 71.5%, so the market makes a Montreal win by two goals or more about 28.5%. Over 5.5 goals is 56%. Utah Mammoth v Vancouver Canucks (05:30): Utah −1.5 is 55.3%, a big number for a puck line, and the total is set at 6.5.

College basketball

College basketball's opening weeks bring huge spreads, because big programmes schedule home games against much smaller schools. Tonight Maryland are −34.5 against Alcorn State, Minnesota −34 against Western Illinois, Ohio State −33 against Prairie View A&M, Texas A&M −33 against Southeastern Louisiana and UCLA −31.5 against Long Beach State. Even at those margins the market prices each spread close to 50/50.

The closer games: Missouri State v Abilene Christian (−5.5, exactly 50/50), Tulsa v Western Kentucky (−6.5) and Drake v Tarleton State (−9). Kent State v Eastern Kentucky has the highest college total at 162.

The BetMeisters tip: Virginia Tech v Richmond · 03:00

The Number Cruncher Meister's free Meister Special, shown in the box above, is Under 150 points at 2.55. Pinnacle's main total for the game is 153, and its price for under 150 makes it about 45%, a fair price of 2.22. At 2.55 that is an edge of about 15%. The bet wins on 149 points or fewer and the stake comes back on exactly 150; overtime counts. Virginia Tech are 13-point favourites.

What the numbers say overall

Three things stand out from tonight's board. First, the NBA spreads are tight around 50/50 almost everywhere, which is what you expect from the most heavily traded basketball market in the world. When a bookmaker's price on an NBA spread drifts far from the sharp line, it is usually a stale price that will be corrected, not a hidden opportunity.

Second, the totals tell you about pace. Cleveland v Chicago at 241.5 and Washington v Memphis at 232.5 are expected to be high-scoring; Boston v Orlando at 212.5 is expected to be slower and more defensive. A total moves when team news breaks, especially when a key scorer is ruled out, so late changes to the total are often the first sign of a missing player.

Third, college basketball is where bookmakers disagree most at this time of year. Teams have played only a handful of games, many against weak opposition, so the sharp market has less to go on and the gaps between bookmakers are bigger. That is why both of tonight's BetMeisters basketball edges, Virginia Tech v Richmond and Adelaide Lightning in the WNBL, came from lower-profile games rather than the NBA.

How to read US lines

  • Spread: the favourite must win by more than the number (−4.5 means by 5 or more). The underdog covers if it wins or loses by less.
  • Total: combined points, including overtime unless stated.
  • Whole-number lines (−2, 150) can land exactly, and then the stake is returned.
