Ireland v Kosovo, Poland v Sweden and Hayate at Southwell: the Original Combo Meister's treble at 3.85

Two Nations League legs and a short-priced favourite under the lights. It lands about 1 time in 4.

The Original Combo Meister's treble pays 3.85: under 2.5 goals in Ireland v Kosovo, both teams to score in Poland v Sweden, both at 22:45 Kampala time, and Hayate to win the 8pm at Southwell (23:00 Kampala). Original Combo Meister picks are manual; we show the numbers only.

The 3 legs

  • 22:45 · Ireland v Kosovo (UEFA Nations League): Under 2.5 goals at 1.55. Fair chance about 61.1%. What it needs: 2 goals or fewer wins in full; more loses.
  • 22:45 · Poland v Sweden (UEFA Nations League): Both teams to score at 1.533. Fair chance about 64.9%. What it needs: both sides must score.
  • 23:00 · Southwell 20:00 UK · 7 runners (Southwell (AW)): Hayate (win) at 1.62. Fair chance about 60.2%. What it needs: the horse must win the race.

The honest numbers

Multiply the fair chances together and the combo lands about 23.9% of the time: roughly 1 in 4. The stake is 0.5 points.

Before you copy it

  • Small stakes only. Multiples go long runs without landing.
  • Prices move. If a leg has shortened, the whole price drops with it.
  • Never chase. A losing multiple is the normal result, not a near miss.
