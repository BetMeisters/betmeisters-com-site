Weekend football preview: Arsenal v Manchester City, the Merseyside derby, Dortmund v Leverkusen and what the sharp market says

The Premier League's two biggest games are on Sunday. Here is every major fixture from England and Europe's top five leagues this weekend, with the chance of each result from the sharpest prices, what stands out, and how to read the numbers.

This weekend's Premier League round is split: three games on Saturday, six on Sunday, and the two biggest both on Sunday, the Merseyside derby at noon and Arsenal v Manchester City at 4.30pm. Around them, Europe's top leagues serve up Dortmund v Leverkusen, Bayern at Hamburg, Como v Juventus and Real Sociedad v Sevilla. This preview sets out what the sharp market thinks of each, so you can compare it with the prices you are offered.

How to read the numbers

Every chance here comes from Pinnacle's prices with the bookmaker's margin removed. Pinnacle takes big bets from professional bettors and moves its prices quickly, so its numbers are the closest thing betting has to a fair price. To turn a chance into a fair price, divide 100 by it: a 40% chance is a fair price of 2.50, 25% is 4.00, 60% is about 1.67. If a bookmaker offers you more than the fair price, the bet has value; less, and the margin is working against you. This is exactly how BetMeisters finds its tips, and our guide to value betting goes further.

Arsenal v Manchester City (Sunday 4.30pm)

The sharp market makes Arsenal 49% to win, with City and the draw almost level at about 25% and 26%. That is a strong home edge for a meeting between two of the league's best sides: a fair price of about 2.04 on Arsenal, around 4.00 on City and 3.90 on the draw. Compare the draw price you are offered with the fair 3.90.

Everton v Liverpool (Sunday noon)

Liverpool are 55% to win the derby, Everton 20%, the draw 25%. The fair price on Liverpool is about 1.80, on Everton about 5.00. The market also rates three or more goals at 58%.

The rest of Sunday

Brighton are clear favourites at home to Newcastle (62%), and the market expects goals there (63% for three or more). Tottenham are 49% at Sunderland, with the draw at 28%, the highest draw chance in Sunday's English games. Fulham v Bournemouth is the closest call (34% v 39%). Crystal Palace v Hull had no sharp price when we checked.

Saturday in England

Saturday's three Premier League games: Leeds are 65% to beat Coventry, Manchester United 44% at home to Brentford (with Brentford a sizeable 31%), and Aston Villa 48% at Ipswich. In the Championship, Wolves are 63% at Lincoln and Birmingham 65% at home to Bolton, while Wrexham v Portsmouth is rated the lowest-scoring game of the day (40% for three or more goals). Germany: Dortmund v Leverkusen, and Bayern at Hamburg

Sunday evening's Dortmund v Leverkusen is rated 46% v 31%, with a 66% chance of three or more goals, one of the highest of the weekend. On Saturday, Bayern are 78% at Hamburg (fair price about 1.29), Mainz 66% at home to Union Berlin, and Paderborn v Schalke is close to a coin flip (37% v 35%). Freiburg v Leipzig on Sunday is close too (35% v 40%), with 64% for three goals or more.

Italy: Como v Juventus, Roma v Monza

The market makes Como favourites at home to Juventus, 48% to 25%, a reminder that a big name does not make a favourite. Roma are 80% against Monza, the shortest Serie A price of the weekend, with 72% for three or more goals. Napoli are 59% at Sassuolo, and Inter 76% at home to Genoa on Saturday night. On Monday, Atalanta are 52% at Lecce.

Spain: Real Sociedad v Sevilla, Barcelona at Deportivo

Real Sociedad are 45% at home to Sevilla on Sunday night. Valencia v Osasuna is rated one of the tightest, lowest-scoring games of the weekend (a 30% draw, 41% for three goals or more). Athletic Club are 56% at Malaga on Saturday. Barcelona's trip to Deportivo and Celta v Villarreal had no sharp result price when we checked.

France: PSG v Lorient, Auxerre v Marseille

PSG are the shortest favourite in Europe this weekend at 82% against Lorient (fair price about 1.23), with 68% for three or more goals. Marseille are 49% at Auxerre on Sunday, and Lens 58% at Angers on Saturday.

Five things the numbers say

  1. Short favourites carry little value on the result. PSG, Roma, Bayern and Inter are all 76% to 82%. If you want to back them, the handicap or goals markets usually offer more.
  2. Home edge still matters at the top. Arsenal are rated twice as likely to win as City.
  3. Draws in close games. The draw is 26% in Arsenal v City and 28% to 30% in Sunderland v Spurs, Paderborn v Schalke and Valencia v Osasuna. A fair draw price at 28% is about 3.57.
  4. Goals are not spread evenly. Roma v Monza (72%), PSG v Lorient (68%) and Dortmund v Leverkusen (66%) top the list; Wrexham v Portsmouth (40%) and Valencia v Osasuna (41%) sit at the bottom.
  5. Names are not chances. Como are favourites against Juventus, and Brentford are rated better than 3 in 10 at Old Trafford.
